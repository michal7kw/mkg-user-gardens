<h1>Non-Small Cell Lung Carcinoma</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Non-small cell lung carcinoma (NSCLC) accounts for ~85% of all lung cancers. The three major histological subtypes are <strong>adenocarcinoma</strong> (~40%; most common; often peripheral; gland-forming), <strong>squamous cell carcinoma</strong> (~25%; often central; linked to smoking; associated with hypercalcemia), and <strong>large cell carcinoma</strong> (~5%; undifferentiated; aggressive). The distinction from <strong>small cell lung cancer (SCLC)</strong> is critical because NSCLC is generally more treatable and uses different therapeutic approaches.</p>
<p>NSCLC treatment has been <strong>revolutionized</strong> over the past 15 years by two therapeutic breakthroughs:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Targeted therapy:</strong> identification of oncogenic driver mutations allows personalized treatment with oral tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs)</li>
<li><strong>Immunotherapy:</strong> immune checkpoint inhibitors (anti-PD-1/PD-L1) have transformed outcomes for patients without targetable mutations</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Molecular testing (NCCN/ASCO/CAP/AMP mandatory for all advanced NSCLC):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>EGFR</strong> (~15% of US adenocarcinomas; ~50% of Asian never-smokers): <strong>osimertinib</strong> (first-line; FLAURA)</li>
<li><strong>ALK</strong> (~5%; younger, never/light smokers): <strong>alectinib</strong> (first-line; ALEX)</li>
<li><strong>ROS1</strong> (~1-2%): crizotinib or entrectinib</li>
<li><strong>KRAS G12C</strong> (~13% of adenocarcinomas; smokers): <strong>sotorasib</strong> or <strong>adagrasib</strong> (second-line)</li>
<li><strong>BRAF V600E</strong> (~2%): dabrafenib + trametinib</li>
<li><strong>MET exon 14 skipping</strong> (~3%): capmatinib or tepotinib</li>
<li><strong>RET</strong> (~1-2%): selpercatinib or pralsetinib</li>
<li><strong>NTRK</strong> (&lt;1%): larotrectinib or entrectinib</li>
<li><strong>PD-L1 expression</strong> (TPS scoring): guides immunotherapy selection</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Screening (USPSTF 2021):</strong> LDCT for ages 50-80 with 20+ pack-year smoking history, current smoker or quit &lt;15 years.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<p><strong>Stage I (localized):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Surgical resection</strong> (lobectomy with lymphadenectomy; gold standard)</li>
<li><strong>SBRT</strong> (stereotactic body radiation therapy) for medically inoperable patients (excellent local control)</li>
<li><strong>Adjuvant immunotherapy</strong> (atezolizumab or pembrolizumab for PD-L1+ Stage IB-IIIA; IMpower010, KEYNOTE-091)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Stage II-IIIA (resectable):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Surgery + adjuvant chemotherapy (platinum doublet)</li>
<li><strong>Neoadjuvant chemoimmunotherapy</strong> (nivolumab + chemotherapy; CheckMate 816; improved event-free survival)</li>
<li>Adjuvant targeted therapy (osimertinib for EGFR+; ADAURA)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Stage IIIB-IIIC (locally advanced, unresectable):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Concurrent chemoradiation</strong> (platinum-based) + <strong>durvalumab</strong> consolidation (PACIFIC)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Stage IV (metastatic):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Driver mutation positive:</strong>
<ul>
<li>EGFR: osimertinib 80 mg daily (first-line)</li>
<li>ALK: alectinib 600 mg BID (first-line)</li>
<li>ROS1: crizotinib or entrectinib</li>
<li>KRAS G12C: sotorasib or adagrasib (after prior therapy)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>No driver mutation (PD-L1-guided):</strong>
<ul>
<li><strong>PD-L1 TPS &gt;=50%:</strong> pembrolizumab monotherapy (KEYNOTE-024) OR chemoimmunotherapy</li>
<li><strong>PD-L1 TPS 1-49%:</strong> chemoimmunotherapy (pembrolizumab + platinum/pemetrexed)</li>
<li><strong>PD-L1 TPS &lt;1%:</strong> chemoimmunotherapy (pembrolizumab or nivolumab + ipilimumab + chemotherapy)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Second-line:</strong> docetaxel, ramucirumab, pemetrexed (for nonsquamous), or clinical trial</li>
</ul>

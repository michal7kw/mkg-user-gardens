<h1>aluminum</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Measures aluminum body burden; monitored in dialysis patients and in cases of suspected environmental or occupational aluminum exposure. Aluminum accumulation can occur with impaired renal clearance and may contribute to bone disease, anemia, and neurological effects.</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used for dialysis monitoring and for assessing toxic metal exposure in patients with renal impairment or known environmental/occupational aluminum contact.</p>

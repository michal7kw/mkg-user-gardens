<h1>HDL Particle Number</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>HDL particle number (HDL-P) reports the total concentration of HDL particles in circulation, usually measured by nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) lipoprotein profiling or ion-mobility analysis. Unlike standard HDL cholesterol, which reflects only the cholesterol cargo, HDL-P counts the particles themselves and can be discordant with HDL-C. Higher HDL-P is generally associated with more efficient reverse cholesterol transport and lower cardiovascular risk, though the clinical utility of routinely measuring it remains an area of active research.</p>

<h1>Kidney Dysfunction</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Kidney dysfunction encompasses acute kidney injury (often reversible) and chronic kidney disease (often progressive). <strong>Diabetes and hypertension</strong> are the leading causes. Early detection through eGFR and urine albumin screening is critical.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>ACE inhibitor or ARB</strong> for proteinuria</li>
<li><strong>SGLT2 inhibitor</strong> for diabetic CKD</li>
<li><strong>Blood pressure</strong> &lt;130/80 mmHg</li>
<li><strong>Dialysis or transplant</strong> for ESRD</li>
</ul>

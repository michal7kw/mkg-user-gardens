<h1>Aging</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Aging is the progressive decline in physiological function that increases vulnerability to disease. The <strong>hallmarks of aging</strong> framework (Lopez-Otin et al., 2013, expanded 2023) identifies interconnected processes: genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic changes, loss of proteostasis, deregulated nutrient sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, stem cell exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, chronic inflammation, and dysbiosis.</p>
<h2>Interventions</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Rapamycin</strong> (mTOR inhibitor) -- extends lifespan in mice</li>
<li><strong>Metformin</strong> (AMPK activator) -- TAME trial targeting aging</li>
<li><strong>NAD+ precursors</strong> (NMN, NR) -- restore mitochondrial function</li>
<li><strong>Senolytics</strong> (dasatinib + quercetin) -- kill senescent cells</li>
<li><strong>Caloric restriction</strong> -- most robust lifespan intervention across species</li>
<li><strong>Exercise</strong> -- most effective anti-aging intervention in humans</li>
</ul>

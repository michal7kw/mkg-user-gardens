<h1>Recommendations — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Personalized health suggestions derived from your out-of-range biomarkers and the<br />
knowledge graph — supplements, interventions, and lab tests worth considering.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Review each recommendation</strong> — it states the rationale, the biomarker(s)<br />
that triggered it, and an evidence level.</li>
<li><strong>Check safety flags</strong> — contraindications against your current medications are<br />
highlighted automatically.</li>
<li><strong>Add to your stack</strong> — adopt a recommendation directly so the supplement and<br />
relevant biomarkers are tracked together.</li>
<li><strong>Give feedback</strong> — thumb a recommendation up or down. Feedback tunes future<br />
suggestions and demotes things you've dismissed.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>New items appear with a badge on the dashboard and sidebar.</li>
<li>Recommendations are educational, not medical advice — discuss changes with a<br />
clinician, especially around medications.</li>
</ul>

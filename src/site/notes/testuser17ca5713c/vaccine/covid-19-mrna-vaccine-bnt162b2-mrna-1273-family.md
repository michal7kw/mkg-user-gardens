<h1>COVID-19 mRNA Vaccine</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Lipid-nanoparticle-encapsulated mRNA encoding the SARS-CoV-2 spike protein (prefusion-stabilized). The Pfizer-BioNTech (Comirnaty / BNT162b2) and Moderna (Spikevax / mRNA-1273) products use this platform. Updated annually to match circulating variants — current formulations target the JN.1 lineage and descendants (2024-25 season).</p>
<p>The mRNA platform's signature advantages — rapid retargeting and strong immunogenicity — were validated at unprecedented scale during the COVID-19 pandemic, with billions of doses administered globally. Myocarditis signal in young males (12-29) is real but rare and typically mild; the absolute risk of myocarditis from COVID-19 infection itself is substantially higher.</p>

<h1>environmental_allergen_panel_ige</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This panel measures allergen-specific IgE antibodies against common indoor and outdoor environmental (aeroallergen) allergens, including tree, grass, and weed pollens, molds, house dust mites, and animal dander. Detectable specific IgE indicates sensitization that, combined with clinical history, supports the diagnosis of environmental allergy.</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used in the evaluation of allergic rhinitis and environmental allergy to identify sensitization to specific aeroallergens.</p>

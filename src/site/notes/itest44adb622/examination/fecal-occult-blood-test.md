<h1>Fecal Occult Blood Test</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The fecal occult blood test (FOBT) detects small amounts of blood in the stool that are not visible to the eye — an early warning sign of colorectal polyps or cancer. The modern <strong>faecal immunochemical test (FIT)</strong> specifically detects human haemoglobin and is the standard for organized screening.</p>
<p>In <strong>Italy</strong>, the <em>ricerca del sangue occulto nelle feci</em> is the free organized SSN colorectal-screening test for adults aged <strong>50-69</strong> (extended to <strong>74</strong> in several regions), repeated every <strong>2 years</strong>. The local <strong>ASL</strong> invites eligible residents and provides a free home-collection kit; a <strong>positive</strong> result is followed by a free <strong>colonoscopy</strong> (no <em>ticket</em>).</p>

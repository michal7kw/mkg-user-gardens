<h1>Chronic Inflammation Explained</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Inflammation has a bad reputation, but in short bursts it is exactly what you want: it is how the body fights infection and repairs injury. The problem is the <em>other</em> kind — a low-grade <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/pathway/inflammation-response/" class="internal-link">Inflammation Response</a> that never fully switches off and smoulders for years. This chronic, silent inflammation is now recognized as a common thread running through heart disease, type 2 diabetes, and cognitive decline.</p>
<h2>The Story</h2>
<p>Acute inflammation is loud and brief: redness, heat, swelling, then resolution. Chronic inflammation is quiet and persistent. Instead of a fire that flares and goes out, imagine a pilot light left burning. At the center of it is a master switch inside cells called the <strong><a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/pathway/nf-kb-pathway/" class="internal-link">NF-kB Pathway</a></strong>, which turns on genes for inflammatory messengers like <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/interleukin-6/" class="internal-link">Interleukin 6</a> and <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/tnf-alpha/" class="internal-link">TNF Alpha</a>. When this switch is chronically activated — by visceral fat, a poor diet, gut problems, chronic stress, or simply aging — those messengers circulate continuously and slowly damage tissues.</p>
<p>You can see the embers in bloodwork. <strong><a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/hs-crp/" class="internal-link">Hs Crp</a></strong> (high-sensitivity C-reactive protein) is the workhorse marker; <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/interleukin-6/" class="internal-link">Interleukin 6</a>, <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/tnf-alpha/" class="internal-link">TNF Alpha</a>, <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/esr/" class="internal-link">Esr</a>, <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/fibrinogen/" class="internal-link">Fibrinogen</a>, and <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/ferritin/" class="internal-link">Ferritin</a> add detail. <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/homocysteine/" class="internal-link">Homocysteine</a> ties inflammation to the cardiovascular and cognitive stories. Because the same inflammatory machinery operates everywhere, the <em>same</em> fire shows up as plaque in arteries (<a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/condition/atherosclerosis/" class="internal-link">Atherosclerosis</a> → <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/condition/cardiovascular-disease/" class="internal-link">Cardiovascular Disease</a>), as worsening <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/condition/insulin-resistance/" class="internal-link">Insulin Resistance</a> in metabolism, and as <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/pathway/neuroinflammation/" class="internal-link">Neuroinflammation</a> in the brain.</p>
<p>This is why researchers coined the term &quot;inflammaging&quot;: chronic low-grade inflammation is now considered a driver of disease <em>across the whole life span</em>, not a side effect of it (PMID: 31806905).</p>
<h2>What This Means For You</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Track the embers.</strong> A simple <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/labtest/hs-crp/" class="internal-link">Hs Crp</a> is one of the most informative, low-cost markers you can follow; persistently elevated values flag the quiet fire even when you feel fine.</li>
<li><strong>What cools it down.</strong> <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/supplement/omega-3-fatty-acids/" class="internal-link">Omega-3 Fatty Acids</a> supply the raw materials for the body's <em>resolution</em> signals; a <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/intervention/mediterranean-diet/" class="internal-link">Mediterranean Diet</a> is consistently anti-inflammatory; and <a href="/garden/e2euserf36a62f1/intervention/exercise/" class="internal-link">Exercise</a> lowers inflammatory markers over time. Reducing visceral fat removes one of the biggest sources of the signal.</li>
<li><strong>It connects everything.</strong> If you have read the metabolic and cardiovascular stories, inflammation is the shared mechanism underneath them — which is why one set of habits improves so many different numbers at once.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Key Takeaways</h2>
<ul>
<li>Acute inflammation heals; chronic low-grade inflammation slowly harms.</li>
<li>The NF-kB switch drives messengers (IL-6, TNF-α) that you can measure via hs-CRP.</li>
<li>The same fire shows up as artery plaque, insulin resistance, and brain inflammation.</li>
<li>Omega-3s, a Mediterranean diet, exercise, and less visceral fat cool it down.</li>
</ul>

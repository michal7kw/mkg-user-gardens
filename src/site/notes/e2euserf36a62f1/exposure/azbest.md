<h1>Azbest</h1>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>Asbestos is a group of naturally occurring fibrous silicate minerals. Chronic inhalation of asbestos fibers causes mesothelioma, lung cancer, and asbestosis (pulmonary fibrosis). Banned or restricted in most countries due to severe carcinogenic properties.</p>

<h1>food_allergen_panel_ige</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This panel measures allergen-specific IgE antibodies directed against a set of common food allergens (such as milk, egg, peanut, tree nuts, soy, wheat, fish, and shellfish). Detectable specific IgE indicates sensitization, which is interpreted alongside clinical history to assess IgE-mediated food allergy.</p>

<h1>hiv_antigen_antibody</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The HIV antigen-antibody combination test is a fourth-generation immunoassay that simultaneously detects the HIV-1 p24 antigen and antibodies to HIV-1 and HIV-2. It is the recommended first-line screening assay because antigen detection shortens the diagnostic window period compared with antibody-only tests.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>
<p>This test measures: HIV 1/2 antigen &amp; antibody.</p>
<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used for HIV screening, including routine and risk-based testing, and as part of the recommended diagnostic algorithm for suspected acute or chronic HIV infection.</p>

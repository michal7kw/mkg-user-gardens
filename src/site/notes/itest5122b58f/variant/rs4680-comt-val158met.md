<h1>rs4680 (COMT Val158Met)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Functional missense variant in COMT (catechol-O-methyltransferase). The Met (A) allele reduces enzyme activity by ~75% and is more thermolabile, slowing prefrontal dopamine clearance. The &quot;warrior/worrier&quot; hypothesis (Stein et al. 2006) proposes that Met/Met carriers (&quot;worriers&quot;) show better working memory under low stress but worse performance under acute stress; Val/Val carriers (&quot;warriors&quot;) show the opposite pattern. Effect sizes in modern large-scale studies are small (&lt;1% of variance) — popular consumer-genetics narratives substantially overstate the impact.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐ (3/5) — robust biochemistry, modest behavioral effects</p>

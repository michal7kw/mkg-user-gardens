<h1>Brain Explorer — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>A focused explorer for brain health — regions, neurotransmitter systems, and the<br />
supplements that target them. It has several sub-pages.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Explorer</strong> — an interactive brain region map; click a region to see the<br />
neurotransmitters and supplements that affect it.</li>
<li><strong>My Stack</strong> — which brain regions your current supplements cover, and the<br />
coverage gaps.</li>
<li><strong>Pathways</strong> — neuro-biochemical pathway diagrams between regions.</li>
<li><strong>Simulate</strong> — run interventions on brain function.</li>
<li><strong>Discover</strong> — ML-powered link prediction for new supplement–region<br />
connections.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>The My Stack and coverage views depend on your supplement stack — populate it<br />
first.</li>
<li>Discover surfaces <em>predicted</em> links — treat them as leads to investigate.</li>
</ul>

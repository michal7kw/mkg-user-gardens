<h1>Mammography</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Mammography uses low-dose X-rays to image breast tissue and detect tumours, microcalcifications, and architectural distortions before they can be felt. It is the foundation of organized breast-cancer screening worldwide.</p>
<p>In <strong>Italy</strong>, screening mammography (<em>mammografia</em>) is a free organized SSN programme: women aged <strong>50-69</strong> are invited every <strong>2 years</strong> (several regions extend this to <strong>45-74</strong>). Invitations are sent by the local <strong>ASL</strong> — no prescription (<em>ricetta</em>) and no <em>ticket</em> are required. Mammography ordered to investigate a symptom (diagnostic, rather than screening) follows the standard <em>medico di base</em> → <em>ricetta</em> → CUP pathway and may carry a ticket.</p>

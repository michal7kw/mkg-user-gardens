<h1>pfas_panel</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This panel quantifies per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), a large family of synthetic &quot;forever chemicals&quot; that persist in the body and environment, in serum or plasma. Because PFAS such as PFOA and PFOS have long half-lives, measured concentrations reflect cumulative environmental and occupational exposure.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>
<p>This test measures: PFAS (PFOA, PFOS, etc.).</p>
<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to assess environmental PFAS exposure, particularly in individuals with known or suspected exposure through contaminated water, food, or occupational sources.</p>

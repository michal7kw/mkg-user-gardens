<h1>immunoglobulin_a_iga</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test quantifies the total concentration of Immunoglobulin A (IgA) in serum. IgA is the principal antibody class found in mucosal secretions and is a key component of humoral immunity. The test is frequently ordered to evaluate immune status and, critically, to confirm that a patient has sufficient IgA before interpreting IgA-based celiac disease serology.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>
<p>This test measures: Immunoglobulin A (IgA).</p>
<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used in immunodeficiency evaluation and for celiac serology validation, where ruling out selective IgA deficiency is necessary to correctly interpret IgA-based antibody screening.</p>

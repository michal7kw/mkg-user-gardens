<h1>Biomarkers — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Where you record and review your biomarker readings over time. Each biomarker<br />
shows a status against reference ranges and a trend from your recent readings.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Add a reading</strong> — search from 100+ supported biomarkers, then enter value,<br />
unit, and date. Each reading is plotted on a time-series chart.</li>
<li><strong>Read the status badge</strong> — optimal / normal / borderline / critical, based on<br />
reference ranges.</li>
<li><strong>Read the trend arrow</strong> — improving, stable, or declining, from your recent<br />
readings.</li>
<li><strong>Convert units</strong> with the built-in unit converter when your lab reports in a<br />
different unit than the chart.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Values outside optimal ranges generate dashboard alerts and feed your<br />
recommendations.</li>
<li>Enter readings consistently (same fasting state, same lab where possible) so<br />
trends are meaningful.</li>
</ul>

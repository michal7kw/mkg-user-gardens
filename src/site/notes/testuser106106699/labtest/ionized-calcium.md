<h1>ionized_calcium</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Ionized calcium is the free, physiologically active fraction of calcium in the blood that is not bound to proteins such as albumin. This test directly measures that free fraction, providing a more accurate assessment of calcium status than total calcium when protein levels or acid-base balance are abnormal. It is ordered to evaluate calcium disorders, parathyroid disease, and patients who are critically ill.</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to evaluate calcium disorders, parathyroid disease, and critical illness, particularly when albumin abnormalities or acid-base disturbances make total calcium unreliable.</p>

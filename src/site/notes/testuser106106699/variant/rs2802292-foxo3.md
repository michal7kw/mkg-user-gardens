<h1>rs2802292 (FOXO3)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>rs2802292 is an intronic variant in <a href="/garden/testuser106106699/gene/foxo3/" class="internal-link">FOXO3</a>, one of only two genes (alongside APOE) consistently associated with human longevity across diverse populations. The G allele is enriched in long-lived individuals and centenarians. Unlike many longevity associations that remain statistical, rs2802292 has a worked-out mechanism: it sits in an intronic enhancer that becomes accessible under cellular stress, where the heat-shock factor HSF1 binds and drives stress-dependent upregulation of FOXO3 — boosting the transcription of antioxidant, autophagy, and DNA-repair genes.</p>

<h2>Clinical Significance</h2>
<p>A protective/longevity-associated variant rather than a disease risk allele. It links FOXO3-mediated stress resistance — including <a href="/garden/testuser106106699/pathway/autophagy/" class="internal-link">Autophagy</a> and antioxidant defense — to healthy aging, and connects genetically to the same nutrient-sensing/longevity programs engaged by caloric restriction and sirtuin signaling.</p>

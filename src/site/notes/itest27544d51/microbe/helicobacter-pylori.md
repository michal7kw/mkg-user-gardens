<h1>Helicobacter pylori</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Spiral-shaped gastric pathogen colonizing roughly half of the global population. Causal in peptic ulcer disease and gastric MALT lymphoma; major risk factor for gastric adenocarcinoma (WHO Group 1 carcinogen since 1994). Survives the acidic stomach by producing urease, which converts urea to ammonia and locally neutralizes pH. Eradication is first-line therapy for confirmed infection with ulcer or atrophic gastritis.</p>

</ul>

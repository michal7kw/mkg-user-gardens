<h1>rs1801133 (MTHFR C677T)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Common functional variant in MTHFR (methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase). The T allele encodes a thermolabile enzyme with ~30% reduced activity in heterozygotes (CT) and ~65% reduced activity in homozygotes (TT). Associated with mildly elevated homocysteine, reduced folate-to-methylfolate conversion, and modest increases in NTD and CVD risk on folate-deficient diets. Effect is largely abolished by adequate folate intake (post-mandatory-fortification studies show diminished CVD signal in fortified populations).</p>
<p><strong>Important caveat:</strong> clinical significance is widely overstated in the consumer-genetics space. ACMG (2013) recommends <em>against</em> routine MTHFR testing — modest effect, no actionable change in management beyond ensuring adequate folate intake, and risk of unnecessary methylfolate supplementation.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)</p>

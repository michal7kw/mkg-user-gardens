<h1>Prevotella copri</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Plant-fiber-degrading gut bacterium dominant in non-Western, high-carbohydrate diet populations (the classic &quot;Prevotella vs Bacteroides&quot; enterotype dichotomy). Conflicting clinical signal: increased abundance is associated with new-onset rheumatoid arthritis (especially in HLA-DRB1 shared-epitope carriers, Scher 2013) and with insulin resistance via branched-chain amino acid biosynthesis (Pedersen 2016), yet other studies show improved glucose tolerance after high-fiber barley intervention is mediated by Prevotella expansion (Kovatcheva-Datchary 2015). Likely reflects strain-level heterogeneity not captured by 16S rRNA.</p>

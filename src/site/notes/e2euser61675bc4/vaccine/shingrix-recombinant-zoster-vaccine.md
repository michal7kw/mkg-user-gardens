<h1>Shingrix (Recombinant Zoster Vaccine)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Recombinant subunit zoster vaccine using varicella-zoster virus glycoprotein E antigen with AS01B adjuvant (a liposomal MPL+QS-21 system). Highly effective (&gt;90% efficacy across all age groups) at preventing herpes zoster and post-herpetic neuralgia. Replaced live-attenuated Zostavax (which had ~50% efficacy and waning protection) as the ACIP-preferred option in 2017. Recommended for all adults ≥50 and for immunocompromised adults ≥19 (where the live Zostavax was contraindicated).</p>
<p>Notable for higher reactogenicity than most vaccines — most patients experience local pain plus systemic symptoms (myalgia, fatigue, headache) lasting 1-2 days. Counsel patients to schedule the second dose strategically.</p>

<h1>tissue_transglutaminase_iga</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test measures anti-tissue transglutaminase IgA (tTG-IgA) antibodies in serum. These autoantibodies target the tissue transglutaminase enzyme and are highly sensitive and specific for celiac disease in patients consuming gluten with normal total IgA. It is the recommended first-line serologic screening test for celiac disease.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>
<p>This test measures: Tissue transglutaminase IgA antibody.</p>
<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used as the primary screening test for celiac disease. Interpretation requires adequate total IgA, so it is typically paired with a total IgA measurement to detect selective IgA deficiency.</p>

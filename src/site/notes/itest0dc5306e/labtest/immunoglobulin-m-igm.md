<h1>immunoglobulin_m_igm</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test quantifies the total concentration of Immunoglobulin M (IgM) in serum. IgM is the first antibody class produced during a primary immune response and serves as an early marker of acute or recent infection. The test is used to assess the acute humoral immune response and to help evaluate immune function.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to investigate acute infection, where elevated IgM indicates a recent or primary immune response, and in the evaluation of immunodeficiency.</p>

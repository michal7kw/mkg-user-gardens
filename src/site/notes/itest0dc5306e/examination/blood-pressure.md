<h1>Blood Pressure</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Blood pressure is the force exerted by circulating blood against arterial walls, expressed as <strong>systolic</strong> (peak contraction) over <strong>diastolic</strong> (minimum relaxation) in mmHg. Normal is <strong>&lt;120/80</strong>; hypertension is <strong>&gt;=130/80</strong> (ACC/AHA 2017). Elevated BP is the <strong>leading modifiable risk factor</strong> for cardiovascular disease worldwide.</p>
<h2>Measurement</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Office</strong> -- seated, 5 min rest, proper cuff, average of 2+ readings</li>
<li><strong>Home monitoring</strong> -- validated upper-arm device; morning and evening</li>
<li><strong>Ambulatory (ABPM)</strong> -- 24-hour; gold standard; detects white-coat and masked HTN</li>
</ul>

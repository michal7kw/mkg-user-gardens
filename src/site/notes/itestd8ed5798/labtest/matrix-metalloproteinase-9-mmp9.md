<h1>matrix_metalloproteinase_9_mmp9</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Matrix metalloproteinase-9 (MMP-9) is a zinc-dependent enzyme that degrades extracellular matrix components and participates in tissue remodeling, inflammation, and vascular wall changes. The MMP-9 test measures circulating enzyme levels in blood. Elevated MMP-9 is observed in inflammatory conditions, in chronic inflammatory response syndrome (CIRS), and in cardiovascular and vascular remodeling processes.</p>

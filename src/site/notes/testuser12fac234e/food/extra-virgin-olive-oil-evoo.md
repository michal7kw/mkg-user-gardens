<h1>Extra Virgin Olive Oil (EVOO)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>First cold-pressed olive oil with free acidity ≤0.8% (the regulatory definition of &quot;extra virgin&quot;). Centerpiece of the Mediterranean diet pattern. Composition: ~73% monounsaturated fat (oleic acid), 14% saturated, 11% polyunsaturated. The bioactive minor components — oleocanthal (an ibuprofen-like COX inhibitor), hydroxytyrosol, oleuropein, tyrosol — are responsible for many of the health effects beyond the favorable fatty-acid profile.</p>
<p>The active arm of <a href="/garden/testuser12fac234e/research/predimed-2018-mediterranean-diet-for-primary-cv-prevention/" class="internal-link">PREDIMED 2018 Mediterranean Diet</a> used 1 L/week of EVOO and showed ~31% relative reduction in major CV events. Polyphenol content varies enormously (0-1000 mg/kg) by cultivar, harvest, and processing — high-polyphenol EVOO has measurably more anti-inflammatory effect.</p>
<p>NOVA class 2 (culinary ingredient) — but EVOO is one of the few class-2 ingredients consistently associated with health <em>benefit</em> rather than risk.</p>

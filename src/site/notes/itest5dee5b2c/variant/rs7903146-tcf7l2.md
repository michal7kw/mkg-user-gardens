<h1>rs7903146 (TCF7L2)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>rs7903146 is an intronic single-nucleotide polymorphism in <a href="/garden/itest5dee5b2c/gene/tcf7l2/" class="internal-link">TCF7L2</a> and the most robustly replicated common variant for type 2 diabetes risk in humans. Each copy of the T (risk) allele raises T2D risk by roughly 30-40%, an effect seen consistently across ancestries. Mechanistically the risk allele impairs glucose-stimulated insulin secretion and blunts the incretin response, implicating beta-cell dysfunction rather than peripheral resistance as the primary driver.</p>

<h2>Clinical Significance</h2>
<p>The strongest common T2D risk variant; also associated with gestational diabetes and with attenuated response to sulfonylureas/incretin therapies. A single-variant exemplar of how T2D genetic risk concentrates in insulin-secretion pathways.</p>

<h1>EMPA-REG OUTCOME 2015 — Empagliflozin in Type 2 Diabetes with Cardiovascular Disease</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Landmark RCT (n=7,020) showing empagliflozin (an SGLT2 inhibitor) reduced cardiovascular death by 38%, all-cause mortality by 32%, and heart failure hospitalization by 35% in patients with T2D and established cardiovascular disease. Reframed SGLT2 inhibitors as cardiovascular drugs, not just glycemic-control agents — one of the most consequential cardiology trials of the 2010s.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5) — large multicenter RCT, mortality endpoint</p>

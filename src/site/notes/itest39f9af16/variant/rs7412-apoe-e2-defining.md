<h1>rs7412 (APOE-e2 defining)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Second of the two APOE-defining SNVs. The T allele (combined with rs429358 T) defines the APOE-e2 haplotype, which is associated with ~50% reduced Alzheimer's disease risk but markedly increased risk of type III hyperlipoproteinemia (familial dysbetalipoproteinemia, ~5% penetrance in e2/e2 homozygotes — requires a &quot;second hit&quot; such as obesity or hypothyroidism to manifest).</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)</p>

<h1>Liver Dysfunction</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Liver dysfunction encompasses any impairment of metabolic, synthetic, and detoxification functions. <strong>NAFLD</strong> is increasingly common (~25% of adults). Management focuses on treating the underlying cause and preventing cirrhosis.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Treat underlying cause</strong> (antivirals, alcohol cessation, weight loss for NAFLD)</li>
<li><strong>Avoid hepatotoxic drugs</strong> and alcohol</li>
<li><strong>Manage complications</strong> (ascites, encephalopathy, varices)</li>
<li><strong>Liver transplantation</strong> for end-stage disease</li>
<li><strong>HCC surveillance</strong> (ultrasound +/- AFP every 6 months in cirrhosis)</li>
</ul>

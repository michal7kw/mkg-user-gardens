<h1>trichomoniasis_naat</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This nucleic acid amplification test (NAAT) detects the genetic material of Trichomonas vaginalis, the protozoan parasite that causes trichomoniasis, the most common curable sexually transmitted infection. NAAT is the most sensitive method for diagnosis, outperforming wet mount microscopy and culture.</p>

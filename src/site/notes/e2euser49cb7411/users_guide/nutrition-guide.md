<h1>Nutrition — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Diet tracking plus tools to connect dietary patterns to your biomarkers. It has<br />
four tabs: <strong>Daily Log</strong>, <strong>Diet Patterns</strong>, <strong>Insights</strong>, and <strong>Nutrient<br />
Browse</strong>.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Log meals</strong> under Daily Log to build a picture of your intake.</li>
<li><strong>Compare diet patterns</strong> (Mediterranean, low-carb, …) under Diet Patterns.</li>
<li><strong>Read Insights</strong> — biomarker-driven dietary recommendations that link your<br />
out-of-range markers to specific changes and nutrients.</li>
<li><strong>Browse nutrients</strong> and their food sources under Nutrient Browse.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Insights get sharper as you track more biomarkers — the page turns lab data into<br />
concrete food guidance.</li>
</ul>

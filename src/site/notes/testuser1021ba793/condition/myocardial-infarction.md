<h1>Myocardial Infarction</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>A life-threatening condition where blood flow to the heart muscle is abruptly cut off, causing tissue death, usually due to a blood clot in a coronary artery.</p>
<h2>Symptoms</h2>
<ul>
<li>Crushing substernal chest pain radiating to left arm, jaw, back</li>
<li>Diaphoresis, nausea, dyspnea, sense of doom</li>
<li>Atypical in women, elderly, diabetics</li>
</ul>
<h2>Treatment Approaches</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Acute (STEMI)</strong>: Door-to-balloon PCI &lt;90 min, aspirin, heparin, P2Y12 inhibitor</li>
<li><strong>Post-MI</strong>: Dual antiplatelet (12 months), high-intensity statin, beta-blocker, ACE inhibitor</li>
<li><strong>Cardiac rehabilitation</strong>: Supervised exercise -- reduces mortality 20-25%</li>
<li><strong>Secondary prevention</strong>: Smoking cessation, Mediterranean diet, BP/glucose control</li>
<li><strong>Supplements</strong>: Omega-3 (REDUCE-IT), CoQ10, magnesium</li>
</ul>

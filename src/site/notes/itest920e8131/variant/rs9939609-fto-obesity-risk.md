<h1>rs9939609 (FTO obesity-risk)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Intronic variant in FTO (originally &quot;fat mass and obesity-associated&quot;) and the most strongly replicated common variant for adult BMI (Frayling et al. 2007). Each A allele is associated with ~1.5 kg higher body weight on average and ~20-30% increased odds of obesity in adults. Mechanism is now believed not to involve FTO itself but cis-regulation of nearby IRX3 and IRX5 expression in adipocyte precursors, shifting their developmental fate from energy-dissipating beige adipocytes toward energy-storing white adipocytes (Claussnitzer et al. NEJM 2015).</p>

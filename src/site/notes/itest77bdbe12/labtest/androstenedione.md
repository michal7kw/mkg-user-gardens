<h1>androstenedione</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Androstenedione is a steroid hormone produced by the adrenal glands and gonads that serves as a precursor in the biosynthesis of both testosterone and estrone. Measuring serum androstenedione helps quantify androgen production and is commonly ordered as part of the workup for excess androgen activity. Elevated levels can point to adrenal or ovarian sources of androgen excess.</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used in the evaluation of polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), hyperandrogenism, and adrenal disorders such as congenital adrenal hyperplasia or androgen-secreting tumors.</p>

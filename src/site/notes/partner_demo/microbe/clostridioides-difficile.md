<h1>Clostridioides difficile</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Spore-forming anaerobe (formerly Clostridium difficile, reclassified 2016) that causes pseudomembranous colitis, typically following antibiotic-induced disruption of the gut microbiota. Toxins A (TcdA) and B (TcdB) inactivate Rho-family GTPases in colonocytes, causing cell rounding, tight-junction disruption, and intense inflammation. Spores are environmentally persistent and resistant to alcohol-based hand sanitizers (soap and water required). Recurrent infection (&gt;2 episodes) is now a primary indication for fecal microbiota transplantation (FMT), which has 80-90% cure rates.</p>

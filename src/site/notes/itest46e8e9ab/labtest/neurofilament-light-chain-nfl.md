<h1>neurofilament_light_chain_nfl</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test measures neurofilament light chain (NfL), a structural protein of neuronal axons that is released into the cerebrospinal fluid and blood when axons are damaged. NfL is a sensitive but non-specific marker of neuro-axonal injury, with elevated levels seen across a range of neurodegenerative diseases, multiple sclerosis, and traumatic brain injury. It is used to detect and monitor the intensity of ongoing neuronal damage.</p>

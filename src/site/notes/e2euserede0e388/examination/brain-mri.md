<h1>Brain MRI</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Magnetic resonance imaging of the brain. Gold-standard non-ionizing imaging for stroke (DWI), multiple sclerosis (FLAIR), tumors (T1+contrast), and most parenchymal pathology. Higher soft-tissue resolution than CT but contraindicated with non-MRI-compatible implants and significantly more expensive and time-consuming. Standard sequences include T1, T2, FLAIR, DWI/ADC, GRE/SWI; gadolinium-enhanced T1 added when neoplasm or inflammation suspected.</p>
<h2>Procedure Details</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Modality:</strong> Imaging</li>
<li><strong>Invasiveness:</strong> Non-invasive</li>
<li><strong>Typical Duration:</strong> 45 minutes</li>
<li><strong>Radiation Dose:</strong> None (uses magnetic fields and radio waves, not ionizing radiation)</li>
</ul>

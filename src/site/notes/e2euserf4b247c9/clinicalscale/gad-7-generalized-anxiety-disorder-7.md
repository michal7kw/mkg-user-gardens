<h1>GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Seven-item self-report screening instrument for generalized anxiety disorder, scored 0-21. Developed by Spitzer et al. (2006) as a companion to the PHQ-9. Cutoff of ≥10 has sensitivity 89% and specificity 82% for GAD. While designed for GAD, it is reasonably sensitive (66-83%) to panic disorder, social phobia, and PTSD — making it a practical general anxiety screen in primary care.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)</p>

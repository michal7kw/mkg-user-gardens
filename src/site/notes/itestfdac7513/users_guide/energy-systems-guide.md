<h1>Energy Systems — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>An educational view of how your body produces and uses energy during exercise.<br />
It has four tabs covering the energy systems, fuel sources, muscle and<br />
performance, and fatigue and recovery.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Energy Systems &amp; ATP</strong> — the three pathways (phosphagen, glycolytic,<br />
oxidative).</li>
<li><strong>Fuel Sources</strong> — carbs, fats, and proteins: storage and ATP yield.</li>
<li><strong>Muscle &amp; Performance</strong> — fiber types and heart-rate training zones.</li>
<li><strong>Fatigue &amp; Recovery</strong> — recovery timeline, EPOC, glycogen replenishment.</li>
<li><strong>Show My Data</strong> to overlay your VO2 max, lactate, and recovery markers<br />
alongside the science.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>This page is educational first — use <em>Show My Data</em> to make it personal.</li>
</ul>

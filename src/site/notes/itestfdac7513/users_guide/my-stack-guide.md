<h1>My Stack — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Your active supplements and the biomarkers you're tracking, together in one<br />
place. You can also adopt curated health stacks built for specific goals.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Browse curated stacks</strong> by goal (energy, sleep, cognition, …). Each bundles<br />
recommended supplements with dosages plus biomarkers to track progress.</li>
<li><strong>Adopt a stack</strong> to add its items to your own stack in one step.</li>
<li><strong>Build your own</strong> — add supplements with dosage and schedule, and pin the<br />
biomarkers you'll watch to judge whether it's working.</li>
<li><strong>Manage lifecycle</strong> — pause, resume, or discontinue a supplement as your<br />
regimen changes.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Pair each supplement with a biomarker so you can actually measure effect.</li>
<li>Adopting a stack doesn't replace medical advice — check interactions on the<br />
Drug Interactions page if you take medications.</li>
</ul>

<h1>Lung Neoplasms</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Lung neoplasms encompass both benign (hamartoma, adenoma) and malignant tumors of the lung. <strong>Lung cancer</strong> (malignant neoplasm) is the <strong>leading cause of cancer death worldwide</strong>, responsible for approximately 1.8 million deaths annually. It is broadly divided into <strong>non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC, ~85%)</strong> and <strong>small cell lung cancer (SCLC, ~15%)</strong> based on histology, treatment approach, and prognosis.</p>
<p><strong>NSCLC subtypes:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Adenocarcinoma</strong> (~40%): most common subtype; often peripheral; EGFR mutations (especially never-smokers, Asian populations)</li>
<li><strong>Squamous cell carcinoma</strong> (~25%): central; often causes cavitation; associated with hypercalcemia (PTHrP)</li>
<li><strong>Large cell carcinoma</strong> (~5%): undifferentiated; aggressive</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>SCLC</strong> is the most aggressive subtype, characterized by rapid growth, early metastasis, and strong association with smoking. It frequently presents with paraneoplastic syndromes (SIADH, Lambert-Eaton, Cushing).</p>
<p><strong>Tobacco smoking</strong> accounts for ~85% of lung cancer deaths. The remaining ~15% of cases (never-smokers) are increasingly recognized as a distinct entity driven by targetable molecular alterations (EGFR, ALK, ROS1) -- these patients respond well to targeted therapy.</p>
<p>The introduction of <strong>low-dose CT (LDCT) screening</strong> for high-risk individuals (National Lung Screening Trial, NELSON) reduces lung cancer mortality by ~20-25%. <strong>Molecular profiling</strong> (EGFR, ALK, ROS1, PD-L1) is now standard for advanced NSCLC, guiding targeted therapy and immunotherapy selection.</p>
<h2>Clinical Presentation</h2>
<p><strong>Early stage (often asymptomatic):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Detected incidentally on imaging or through screening</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Locally advanced:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Persistent or worsening cough</li>
<li>Hemoptysis</li>
<li>Chest pain</li>
<li>Dyspnea</li>
<li>Hoarseness (recurrent laryngeal nerve palsy)</li>
<li>Wheezing, stridor</li>
<li>Recurrent post-obstructive pneumonia</li>
<li>Pleural effusion</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Metastatic/advanced:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Weight loss, anorexia, fatigue</li>
<li>Bone pain, pathologic fracture</li>
<li>Neurologic symptoms (brain metastases)</li>
<li>Jaundice (liver metastases)</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Paraneoplastic syndromes:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>SIADH (SCLC; hyponatremia)</li>
<li>Hypercalcemia (squamous; PTHrP)</li>
<li>Cushing syndrome (SCLC; ectopic ACTH)</li>
<li>Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome (SCLC)</li>
<li>Hypertrophic pulmonary osteoarthropathy (NSCLC; clubbing)</li>
</ul>
<h2>Diagnostic Information</h2>
<p><strong>Imaging:</strong> CT chest with contrast; PET-CT for staging; MRI brain (metastasis)<br />
<strong>Tissue diagnosis:</strong> bronchoscopy with biopsy; CT-guided percutaneous biopsy; surgical biopsy<br />
<strong>Molecular testing (NSCLC):</strong> EGFR, ALK, ROS1, BRAF, MET, RET, KRAS, NTRK, PD-L1 expression<br />
<strong>Staging:</strong> TNM 8th edition (T: size/extent, N: nodes, M: metastasis)<br />
<strong>SCLC staging:</strong> limited (confined to one hemithorax) vs extensive (beyond)</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<p><strong>NSCLC:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Stage I-II:</strong> surgical resection (+ adjuvant chemotherapy for II; adjuvant immunotherapy for IIB-III)</li>
<li><strong>Stage III:</strong> concurrent chemoradiation; durvalumab consolidation; surgery for select IIIA</li>
<li><strong>Stage IV (driver mutation):</strong> targeted therapy -- osimertinib (EGFR), alectinib (ALK), crizotinib (ROS1), sotorasib (KRAS G12C)</li>
<li><strong>Stage IV (no driver):</strong> immunotherapy (pembrolizumab/nivolumab ± ipilimumab); chemotherapy for PD-L1-negative</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>SCLC:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Limited stage:</strong> concurrent chemoradiation (etoposide + platinum) + prophylactic cranial irradiation</li>
<li><strong>Extensive stage:</strong> chemotherapy (etoposide + platinum) + atezolizumab/durvalumab</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Supportive:</strong> palliative radiation for pain, brain metastases; pleurodesis for effusion</p>

<h1>Eye Diseases</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Eye diseases encompass a broad spectrum of disorders affecting the eye and its adnexal structures. Globally, <strong>an estimated 2.2 billion people</strong> have visual impairment, of whom at least <strong>1 billion have a preventable or treatable condition</strong> (WHO World Report on Vision, 2019). The most common causes of vision loss worldwide are <strong>uncorrected refractive error</strong> (myopia, hyperopia, astigmatism), <strong>cataract</strong>, <strong>age-related macular degeneration (AMD)</strong>, <strong>glaucoma</strong>, and <strong>diabetic retinopathy</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Major eye disease categories:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Refractive errors:</strong> myopia (nearsightedness), hyperopia (farsightedness), astigmatism, presbyopia. Treated with glasses, contact lenses, or refractive surgery (LASIK).</li>
<li><strong>Cataract:</strong> clouding of the crystalline lens; the leading cause of blindness worldwide. Treated with surgical extraction and intraocular lens implantation (one of the most successful surgical procedures).</li>
<li><strong>Glaucoma:</strong> optic neuropathy causing progressive visual field loss; associated with elevated intraocular pressure. Silent progression; requires regular screening for at-risk individuals.</li>
<li><strong>Age-related macular degeneration (AMD):</strong> degeneration of the macula (central retina); leading cause of vision loss in older adults in developed countries. Neovascular (wet) AMD treated with anti-VEGF injections.</li>
<li><strong>Diabetic retinopathy:</strong> microvascular complication of diabetes; leading cause of blindness in working-age adults. Preventable with glycemic control and screening.</li>
<li><strong>Infections:</strong> conjunctivitis, keratitis, endophthalmitis (see separate entries)</li>
<li><strong>Dry eye disease:</strong> multifactorial ocular surface disease; increasingly common</li>
</ul>

<h3>NOTE</h3>
<p>Lutein and zeaxanthin (macular carotenoids) are clinically important for eye health but do not have entity files in the curated knowledge graph. The AREDS2 formulation (lutein 10 mg + zeaxanthin 2 mg + vitamin C 500 mg + vitamin E 400 IU + zinc 80 mg + copper 2 mg) reduces progression of intermediate AMD by ~25%.</p>

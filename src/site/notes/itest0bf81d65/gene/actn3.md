<h1>ACTN3</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>ACTN3 encodes alpha-actinin-3, an actin-binding structural protein of the sarcomeric Z-disc that is expressed almost exclusively in <strong>fast-twitch (type II) muscle fibers</strong>. It is famous as the &quot;sprint gene&quot; because of a common nonsense polymorphism, <strong>R577X (rs1815739)</strong>: the X (T) allele introduces a premature stop codon, and homozygous XX individuals — about 18% of the population — produce no functional alpha-actinin-3 at all.</p>
<p>Remarkably, this complete deficiency is compatible with normal life (alpha-actinin-2 compensates), but it shifts muscle toward a more oxidative, endurance-favoring phenotype. The functional RR genotype is over-represented among elite sprint/power athletes, while XX is more frequent in endurance athletes — one of the most reproducible gene-performance associations in exercise genomics, though effect sizes at the individual level are modest.</p>

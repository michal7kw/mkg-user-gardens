<h1>Total T3 (Triiodothyronine)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Total T3 measures the total triiodothyronine concentration in blood, including both the protein-bound and free fractions. T3 is the most metabolically active thyroid hormone; the majority is produced outside the thyroid by deiodination of thyroxine (T4). Total T3 is particularly helpful in evaluating suspected hyperthyroidism and T3 toxicosis, and like total T4 it is affected by binding-protein levels. It is best interpreted in the context of TSH and T4 results.</p>

<h1>AI Assistant — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>A chat assistant grounded in the knowledge graph and your own data (retrieval-<br />
augmented generation), so answers stay tied to real entities rather than<br />
guesswork.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Ask a question</strong> in plain language — e.g. &quot;What affects my HbA1c?&quot; or<br />
&quot;Is magnesium safe with my medications?&quot;.</li>
<li><strong>Use suggested prompts</strong> on a fresh chat to get started quickly.</li>
<li><strong>Inspect the sources</strong> — each answer shows the entities it retrieved as a<br />
small subgraph, so you can verify what it's based on and click through.</li>
<li><strong>Start a new chat</strong> to reset context; past sessions are listed in the<br />
sidebar.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>The assistant searches the curated graph plus your tracked data — the more you<br />
track, the more personal its answers.</li>
<li>If the health banner shows degraded retrieval, answers fall back to general<br />
knowledge; try again once retrieval is healthy.</li>
</ul>

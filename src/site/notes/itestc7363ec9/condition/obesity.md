<h1>Obesity</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Obesity is a chronic metabolic disease defined by excessive body fat (BMI &gt;=30 kg/m2). Affecting <strong>over 40% of US adults</strong>, it drives type 2 diabetes, cardiovascular disease, osteoarthritis, sleep apnea, and multiple cancers. The treatment landscape has been transformed by <strong>GLP-1 receptor agonists</strong> -- semaglutide achieves ~15% weight loss, tirzepatide ~20-22%, approaching bariatric surgery results.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Lifestyle</strong> -- Mediterranean diet, 150 min/week exercise, behavioral therapy</li>
<li><strong>Semaglutide</strong> 2.4 mg weekly (Wegovy) -- ~15% weight loss</li>
<li><strong>Tirzepatide</strong> 5-15 mg weekly (Zepbound) -- ~20-22% weight loss</li>
<li><strong>Bariatric surgery</strong> -- sleeve gastrectomy or Roux-en-Y; BMI &gt;=35 with comorbidity</li>
<li><strong>Treat comorbidities</strong> -- diabetes, hypertension, sleep apnea</li>
</ul>

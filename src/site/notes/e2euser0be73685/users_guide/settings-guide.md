<h1>Settings — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Where you customize your MKG experience and manage your account.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Profile</strong> — name, email, avatar.</li>
<li><strong>Security</strong> — password and multi-factor authentication.</li>
<li><strong>Goals</strong> — set health objectives that drive recommendations.</li>
<li><strong>Notifications &amp; Display</strong> — alert preferences, theme, and layout.</li>
<li><strong>AI</strong> — choose your LLM provider for AI features.</li>
<li><strong>Privacy</strong> — control your data.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Your health goals feed the Recommendations engine — keep them current.</li>
<li>Display preferences (theme, layout, view mode) are saved per user and follow<br />
you across devices.</li>
</ul>

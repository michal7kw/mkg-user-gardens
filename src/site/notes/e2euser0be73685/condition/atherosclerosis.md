<h1>Atherosclerosis</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Atherosclerosis is a chronic inflammatory disease of the arterial wall where lipid-laden plaques build up, causing vessel narrowing and thrombosis. It is the pathological substrate for <strong>coronary artery disease, stroke, and peripheral arterial disease</strong> -- collectively the leading cause of death worldwide.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Statins</strong> -- reduce LDL, stabilize plaques</li>
<li><strong>Blood pressure control</strong> (&lt;130/80)</li>
<li><strong>Antiplatelet therapy</strong> (aspirin for secondary prevention)</li>
<li><strong>Smoking cessation</strong> -- the single most effective intervention</li>
<li><strong>Revascularization</strong> (PCI, CABG) for symptomatic disease</li>
</ul>

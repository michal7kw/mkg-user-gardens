<h1>rs1801131 (MTHFR A1298C)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>rs1801131 is the A1298C missense variant in <a class="internal-link is-unresolved" href="/404">MTHFR</a> (methylenetetrahydrofolate reductase), the enzyme that produces the active folate form 5-methyltetrahydrofolate for homocysteine remethylation. The C (variant) allele causes a Glu429Ala substitution that modestly lowers enzyme activity — notably less than the better-known C677T variant (rs1801133). In isolation A1298C is generally clinically silent, but <strong>compound heterozygosity with C677T</strong> can meaningfully reduce folate-cycle throughput and elevate homocysteine.</p>

<h2>Clinical Significance</h2>
<p>A mild activity-reducing variant. Consumer-genetics narratives frequently overstate its impact; the strongest evidence is for combined A1298C/C677T genotypes affecting <a href="/garden/itest492ad440/labtest/homocysteine/" class="internal-link">Homocysteine</a> and folate status. Adequate folate intake (or methylfolate in carriers with elevated homocysteine) is the practical mitigation.</p>

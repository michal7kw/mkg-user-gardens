<h1>SPRINT 2015 — Intensive vs Standard Blood Pressure Control</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>RCT (n=9,361) comparing intensive (SBP target &lt;120 mmHg) vs standard (SBP target &lt;140 mmHg) blood pressure control in non-diabetic adults at high CV risk. Intensive group had 25% lower rate of major CV events and 27% lower all-cause mortality. Trial was stopped early at median 3.3 years follow-up because of clear benefit. Directly led to lowered BP targets in 2017 ACC/AHA guidelines (which redefined hypertension as ≥130/80 mmHg).</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)</p>
<h2>Citation</h2>
<p>SPRINT Research Group, Wright JT Jr, Williamson JD, et al. <em>A Randomized Trial of Intensive versus Standard Blood-Pressure Control.</em> N Engl J Med. 2015;373(22):2103-2116. PMID: <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26551272">26551272</a>.</p>

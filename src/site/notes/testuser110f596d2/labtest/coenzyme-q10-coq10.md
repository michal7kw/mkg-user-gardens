<h1>coenzyme_q10_coq10</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Measures plasma Coenzyme Q10 (ubiquinone), a lipid-soluble cofactor essential to the mitochondrial electron-transport chain and a cellular antioxidant. Levels are often assessed during statin therapy, which can lower endogenous CoQ10, and in contexts of suspected mitochondrial dysfunction or cardiac disease.</p>

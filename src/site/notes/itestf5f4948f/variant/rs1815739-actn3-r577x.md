<h1>rs1815739 (ACTN3 R577X)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>rs1815739 is the famous R577X variant in <a href="/garden/itestf5f4948f/gene/actn3/" class="internal-link">ACTN3</a>, often called the &quot;sprint gene&quot; polymorphism. The T allele converts an arginine codon to a stop codon (R577X), so XX homozygotes — roughly 18% of the global population — make no functional alpha-actinin-3 in their fast-twitch (type II) muscle fibers. Despite this complete deficiency being physiologically well tolerated (alpha-actinin-2 compensates), it nudges muscle toward a more oxidative, endurance-favoring phenotype.</p>

<h2>Clinical Significance</h2>
<p>The RR/RX genotypes (functional alpha-actinin-3) are over-represented among elite sprint and power athletes, while XX is more frequent in elite endurance athletes — one of exercise genomics' most reproducible associations, albeit with small individual-level effect. No disease significance; it is a benign performance-modifying variant.</p>

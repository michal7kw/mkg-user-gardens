<h1>Colorectal Cancer</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Colorectal cancer is a malignancy arising from adenomatous polyps via the <strong>adenoma-carcinoma sequence</strong>. It is the <strong>third most diagnosed cancer</strong> worldwide. <strong>Screening colonoscopy</strong> (starting age 45-50) reduces incidence and mortality by up to 60-70%.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Surgical resection</strong> -- primary treatment</li>
<li><strong>Chemotherapy</strong> -- adjuvant FOLFOX for stage III+</li>
<li><strong>Targeted therapy</strong> -- anti-EGFR for RAS wild-type; anti-VEGF</li>
<li><strong>Immunotherapy</strong> -- pembrolizumab for MSI-H tumors</li>
<li><strong>Screening</strong> -- colonoscopy every 10 years, FIT annually</li>
</ul>

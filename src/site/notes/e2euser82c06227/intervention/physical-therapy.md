<h1>Physical Therapy</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Physical therapy uses therapeutic exercise, manual techniques, and patient education to improve mobility, reduce pain, restore function, and prevent disability. It is first-line for <strong>musculoskeletal conditions</strong> (back pain, osteoarthritis, post-surgical), <strong>neurological conditions</strong> (stroke, Parkinson's), and <strong>cardiopulmonary rehabilitation</strong>.</p>
<h2>Applications</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Musculoskeletal</strong> -- back/neck pain, osteoarthritis, tendinopathy, fracture rehab, joint replacement recovery</li>
<li><strong>Neurological</strong> -- stroke rehabilitation (gait, arm function), balance/vestibular therapy</li>
<li><strong>Cardiopulmonary</strong> -- cardiac rehabilitation (post-MI), pulmonary rehabilitation (COPD)</li>
<li><strong>Sports medicine</strong> -- ACL reconstruction rehab, return-to-play protocols</li>
<li><strong>Pelvic floor</strong> -- incontinence, post-prostatectomy</li>
</ul>

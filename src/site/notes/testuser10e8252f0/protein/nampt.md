<h1>NAMPT</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Nicotinamide phosphoribosyltransferase, the rate-limiting enzyme in the NAD+ salvage pathway. Converts nicotinamide to nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN), representing a critical control point for cellular NAD+ biosynthesis.</p>

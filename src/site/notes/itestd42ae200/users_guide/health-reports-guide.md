<h1>Health Reports — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Comprehensive, AI-generated reports that pull your biomarkers, trends, risks, and<br />
recommendations into one readable summary.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Generate a report</strong> on demand — it synthesises your latest data with<br />
knowledge-graph context.</li>
<li><strong>Read the insights</strong> — the report surfaces what matters most across your<br />
metrics.</li>
<li><strong>Export</strong> as PDF, CSV, or JSON to share with a clinician or keep for your<br />
records.</li>
<li><strong>Compare over time</strong> using the archive of past reports.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Reports are most useful after you've tracked several biomarkers over a few time<br />
points.</li>
<li>Exports are a great way to bring structured data to an appointment.</li>
</ul>

<h1>Dehydration</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Dehydration is a deficit of total body water that impairs normal physiological function. It ranges from mild (thirst, dry mouth) to severe (hypovolemic shock, altered mental status). Common causes include vomiting, diarrhea, fever, excessive sweating, and inadequate intake.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mild</strong> -- oral rehydration with water and electrolytes</li>
<li><strong>Moderate</strong> -- IV normal saline 1-2 L bolus</li>
<li><strong>Severe</strong> -- aggressive IV resuscitation, monitor urine output and electrolytes</li>
<li><strong>Treat underlying cause</strong></li>
</ul>

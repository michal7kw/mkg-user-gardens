<h1>VLDL Cholesterol</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>VLDL cholesterol quantifies the cholesterol fraction carried by very-low-density lipoprotein particles, the main triglyceride-rich lipoproteins secreted by the liver. In most clinical settings VLDL-C is calculated as triglycerides/5 (Friedewald estimation) as part of a standard fasting lipid panel, though direct measurement is available. Because VLDL particles are atherogenic remnants, elevated VLDL-C reflects increased cardiovascular risk and is commonly seen in insulin resistance and metabolic syndrome.</p>

<h1>Hypothyroidism</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Hypothyroidism is a common endocrine disorder where the thyroid produces insufficient hormone, slowing metabolism. It affects ~5% of the population (predominantly women). <strong>Hashimoto thyroiditis</strong> is the leading cause in iodine-sufficient regions.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Levothyroxine</strong> (T4) 1.6 mcg/kg/day -- first-line; titrate to TSH 0.5-2.5 mIU/L</li>
<li><strong>Monitor TSH</strong> every 6-8 weeks until stable, then annually</li>
<li><strong>Pregnancy</strong> -- increase dose 25-30%; target TSH &lt;2.5</li>
</ul>

<h1>Ashwagandha</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Premier adaptogenic herb (Withania somnifera) used for 3,000+ years in Ayurvedic medicine. The most studied adaptogen with robust RCT evidence for cortisol reduction (14-28%), anxiety relief, testosterone increase in men, thyroid support, and improved sleep quality. Active compounds are withanolides (steroidal lactones), with KSM-66 and Sensoril being the two standardized root extracts with the strongest clinical evidence.</p>
<h2>Mechanism of Action</h2>
<p>Withanolides modulate the HPA axis, reducing excessive cortisol production. GABAergic activity contributes to anxiolytic effects. Inhibits NF-kB (anti-inflammatory), enhances thyroid hormone production (T3 and T4), supports DHEA-S levels, and may increase testosterone through cortisol reduction and direct effects on Leydig cells. Also demonstrates acetylcholinesterase inhibitory activity (cognitive benefits).</p>
<h2>Dosage</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>KSM-66</strong> (root extract, most studied): 300-600 mg/day</li>
<li><strong>Sensoril</strong> (root + leaf extract): 125-250 mg/day</li>
<li><strong>Timing</strong>: Evening preferred (sleep benefits), or split AM/PM doses</li>
<li><strong>Duration</strong>: Effects build over 4-8 weeks; cortisol reduction measurable at 2 weeks</li>
<li><strong>Caution</strong>: May increase thyroid hormone levels — monitor in hyperthyroid patients. Nightshade family — avoid if nightshade sensitivity</li>
</ul>

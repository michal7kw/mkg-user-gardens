<h1>Anemia</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Anemia is a reduction in hemoglobin below reference ranges (typically &lt;13 g/dL men, &lt;12 g/dL women). It is the <strong>most common blood disorder globally</strong>, affecting over <strong>1.5 billion people</strong>. Iron deficiency is the leading cause. Classification by MCV (microcytic, normocytic, macrocytic) guides diagnosis.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Iron deficiency</strong> -- oral ferrous sulfate 325 mg TID or IV iron</li>
<li><strong>B12 deficiency</strong> -- 1000 mcg IM or high-dose oral</li>
<li><strong>Folate deficiency</strong> -- folic acid 1-5 mg daily</li>
<li><strong>Anemia of chronic disease</strong> -- treat underlying condition; ESA for CKD</li>
<li><strong>Severe</strong> -- blood transfusion</li>
</ul>

<h1>Acne Vulgaris</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>A common chronic inflammatory skin condition of the pilosebaceous unit, characterized by comedones, papules, pustules, and nodules.</p>
<h2>Symptoms</h2>
<ul>
<li>Comedones (blackheads, whiteheads), papules, pustules, nodules, cysts</li>
<li>Primarily face, chest, back (sebaceous gland-rich areas)</li>
<li>Post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, scarring in severe cases</li>
</ul>
<h2>Treatment Approaches</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mild</strong>: Topical retinoids (adapalene, tretinoin), benzoyl peroxide, salicylic acid</li>
<li><strong>Moderate</strong>: Add topical antibiotics (clindamycin), oral antibiotics (doxycycline short-course)</li>
<li><strong>Severe/cystic</strong>: Isotretinoin (Accutane) -- most effective but requires monitoring</li>
<li><strong>Hormonal (women)</strong>: Spironolactone, combined OCs -- address androgen-driven acne</li>
<li><strong>Diet</strong>: Low glycemic index diet reduces acne; dairy (especially skim milk) may worsen</li>
<li><strong>Supplements</strong>: Zinc (30mg/day), omega-3, probiotics, vitamin A</li>
</ul>

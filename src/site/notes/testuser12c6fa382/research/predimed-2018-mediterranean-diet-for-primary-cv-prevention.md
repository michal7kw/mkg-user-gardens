<h1>PREDIMED 2018 — Mediterranean Diet for Primary CV Prevention</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>RCT (n=7,447) in Spanish adults at high CV risk comparing Mediterranean diet supplemented with either extra-virgin olive oil (1 L/week) or mixed nuts (30 g/day) vs a reduced-fat control diet. Mediterranean diet groups had ~30% relative reduction in major CV events. The trial was originally published in 2013, retracted in 2018 due to randomization protocol deviations at some sites, and re-published with conservative methods that preserved the primary finding. Despite the controversy, it remains the strongest dietary RCT for primary CV prevention — most other dietary trials show only intermediate biomarker benefits, not hard endpoints.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) — strong design but with methodological asterisks</p>
<h2>Citation</h2>
<p>Estruch R, Ros E, Salas-Salvadó J, et al. <em>Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease with a Mediterranean Diet Supplemented with Extra-Virgin Olive Oil or Nuts.</em> N Engl J Med. 2018;378(25):e34. PMID: <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29897866">29897866</a>.</p>

<h1>Influenza Vaccine (Annual)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Annually reformulated vaccine targeting the influenza strains predicted by WHO to circulate in the upcoming season. Available formulations:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>IIV (inactivated influenza vaccine)</strong> — egg-based, most common</li>
<li><strong>RIV (recombinant)</strong> — egg-free, suitable for severe egg allergy</li>
<li><strong>LAIV (live-attenuated, intranasal)</strong> — for healthy non-pregnant ages 2-49</li>
<li><strong>HD-IIV (high-dose)</strong> — 4x antigen for ages ≥65; superior efficacy in older adults</li>
<li><strong>aIIV (MF59-adjuvanted)</strong> — alternative for ≥65</li>
</ul>
<p>All current US formulations are quadrivalent (2 A strains + 2 B strains). Universal annual recommendation since 2010 for all persons ≥6 months.</p>

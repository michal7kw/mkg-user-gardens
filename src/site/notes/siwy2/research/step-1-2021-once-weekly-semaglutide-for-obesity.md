<h1>STEP-1 2021 — Once-Weekly Semaglutide for Obesity</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>RCT (n=1,961) of semaglutide 2.4 mg weekly vs placebo for 68 weeks in adults with obesity (BMI ≥30) without diabetes. Mean weight loss 14.9% with semaglutide vs 2.4% with placebo — comparable to early bariatric surgery results and triple the effect size of any prior pharmacological agent. Triggered the GLP-1 obesity-treatment revolution and led to FDA approval of Wegovy in June 2021.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)</p>
<h2>Citation</h2>
<p>Wilding JPH, Batterham RL, Calanna S, et al. <em>Once-Weekly Semaglutide in Adults with Overweight or Obesity.</em> N Engl J Med. 2021;384(11):989-1002. PMID: <a href="https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33567185">33567185</a>.</p>

<h1>Greek Yogurt (plain, full-fat)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Strained yogurt with much higher protein content than regular yogurt (~10g per 100g vs ~3.5g) due to removal of whey during straining. Live-culture varieties contain Lactobacillus bulgaricus and Streptococcus thermophilus; some brands add Lactobacillus acidophilus or Bifidobacterium probiotic strains (check label — heat-treated yogurts have no live cultures).</p>
<p>Fermentation reduces lactose content to ~4g per 100g (vs ~5g in milk), making most varieties tolerable for individuals with mild lactose intolerance. The protein is approximately 80% casein and 20% whey — both highly bioavailable.</p>

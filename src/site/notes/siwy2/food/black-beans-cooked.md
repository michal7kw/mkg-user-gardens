<h1>Black Beans (cooked)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Mature legume (Phaseolus vulgaris). Excellent source of plant protein (when paired with rice or other complementary protein, becomes &quot;complete&quot;), dietary fiber, folate, magnesium, and polyphenols (anthocyanins responsible for the dark color). Particularly rich in resistant starch and fermentable oligosaccharides — substrates that selectively feed beneficial gut bacteria including <a href="/garden/siwy2/microbe/faecalibacterium-prausnitzii/" class="internal-link">Faecalibacterium prausnitzii</a> and <a href="/garden/siwy2/microbe/roseburia-intestinalis/" class="internal-link">Roseburia intestinalis</a>, producing butyrate.</p>
<p>Low glycemic index (~30) and minimal blood-glucose response despite carbohydrate content. Black beans appear in the Mediterranean diet, MIND diet, DASH diet, and most longevity-focused dietary patterns.</p>

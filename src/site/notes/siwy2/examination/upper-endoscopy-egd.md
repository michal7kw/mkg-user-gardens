<h1>Upper Endoscopy (EGD)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Esophagogastroduodenoscopy — endoscopic visualization of esophagus, stomach, and duodenum using a flexible fiber-optic instrument. First-line test for upper GI bleeding, refractory dyspepsia, dysphagia, suspected celiac disease, and Barrett's esophagus surveillance. Allows diagnostic biopsies and therapeutic interventions (variceal banding, ulcer hemostasis, polyp removal, foreign-body retrieval, dilation of strictures).</p>

<h1>Echocardiogram (Transthoracic)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Ultrasound-based imaging of heart structure and function. First-line test for evaluating heart failure, valvular disease, cardiomyopathy, pericardial effusion, and intracardiac shunts. Provides real-time assessment of ejection fraction (LVEF), chamber sizes, valve function, and wall motion. Standard modes: 2D B-mode, M-mode for chamber dimensions, color Doppler for blood flow, spectral Doppler for valve gradients, tissue Doppler for diastolic function. Strain imaging (speckle tracking) detects subclinical dysfunction.</p>
<p>For technically difficult patients (obesity, lung disease) or higher-resolution assessment of valves and atria, transesophageal echo (TEE) is the alternative.</p>

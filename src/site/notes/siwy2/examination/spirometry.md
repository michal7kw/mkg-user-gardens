<h1>Spirometry</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Pulmonary function test measuring airflow during forced exhalation through a calibrated mouthpiece. Required for diagnosis of asthma and COPD per GINA and GOLD guidelines respectively. Key indices: FEV1 (forced expiratory volume in 1 second), FVC (forced vital capacity), FEV1/FVC ratio, peak expiratory flow, and bronchodilator reversibility (12% AND 200 mL improvement = significant). FEV1/FVC &lt; 0.70 post-bronchodilator defines COPD; bronchodilator-responsive obstruction with normal FVC suggests asthma.</p>

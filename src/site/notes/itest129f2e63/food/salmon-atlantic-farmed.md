<h1>Salmon (Atlantic, farmed)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Fatty cold-water fish, prized for high omega-3 (EPA + DHA) content. A 100g cooked serving of Atlantic farmed salmon contains ~2.15 g combined EPA + DHA — meeting most expert-recommended daily targets in a single serving. Complete protein with all essential amino acids, plus rich in vitamin D (one of the few significant dietary sources), vitamin B12, and selenium.</p>
<p>Wild salmon (chinook, sockeye, coho) has lower total fat but similar absolute EPA + DHA per serving and higher astaxanthin (the carotenoid responsible for the pink color). Farmed salmon has been criticized for higher saturated fat content and PCB/dioxin exposure, but modern farming practices have reduced contamination significantly.</p>

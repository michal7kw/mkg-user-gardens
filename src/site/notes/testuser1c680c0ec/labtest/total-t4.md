<h1>Total T4 (Thyroxine)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Total T4 quantifies the total amount of thyroxine in the bloodstream, encompassing both the fraction bound to carrier proteins (thyroxine-binding globulin, transthyretin, albumin) and the small unbound (free) fraction. Thyroxine is the principal hormone produced by the thyroid gland and serves as a prohormone that is deiodinated peripherally to triiodothyronine (T3). Because total T4 is sensitive to changes in binding-protein concentrations (such as in pregnancy or estrogen therapy), it is typically interpreted together with TSH and free thyroid hormone tests.</p>

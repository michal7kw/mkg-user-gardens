<h1>Cognitive Decline</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Cognitive decline spans a spectrum from <strong>subjective cognitive impairment</strong> (SCI) through <strong>mild cognitive impairment</strong> (MCI) to <strong>dementia</strong>. MCI affects <strong>10-20% of adults over 65</strong> and progresses to dementia at <strong>10-15% per year</strong>. Modifiable risk factors account for ~<strong>40% of dementia risk</strong> per the <strong>Lancet Commission</strong> (12 factors including hearing loss, hypertension, education, physical inactivity).</p>
<h2>Prevention</h2>
<p>The <strong>FINGER trial</strong> showed a multidomain intervention (diet, exercise, cognitive training, vascular risk management) improved cognition. <strong>Hearing aids</strong> reduce decline (ACHIEVE trial). <strong>Exercise</strong> is the strongest individual protective factor.</p>

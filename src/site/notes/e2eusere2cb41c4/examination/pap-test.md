<h1>Pap Test</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The Pap test (Papanicolaou smear, cervical cytology) collects cells from the surface of the cervix and examines them under a microscope for precancerous (dysplastic) or cancerous changes. It is the historical cornerstone of cervical cancer screening.</p>
<p>In <strong>Italy</strong>, cervical screening (<em>screening cervicale</em>) is a free organized SSN programme: women aged <strong>25-30</strong> are invited for a Pap test every <strong>3 years</strong>, and from age <strong>30 to 64</strong> the programme switches to primary <strong>HPV-DNA testing</strong> every <strong>5 years</strong> (with cytology used as a reflex/triage test). Invitations are sent by the local <strong>ASL</strong> — no prescription (<em>ricetta</em>) and no <em>ticket</em> are needed.</p>

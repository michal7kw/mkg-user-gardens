<h1>TCF7L2</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>TCF7L2 (transcription factor 7-like 2) encodes a key effector of the Wnt signaling pathway that, in the pancreas and gut, governs beta-cell proliferation, insulin granule biogenesis, and the incretin axis (proglucagon/<a href="/garden/e2euser0184f85d/hormone/glp-1/" class="internal-link">GLP-1</a> processing). It rose to prominence because its intronic variant <strong>rs7903146</strong> is, across populations, the <strong>strongest common genetic risk factor for type 2 diabetes</strong> identified to date — a finding replicated in essentially every major diabetes GWAS.</p>
<p>The risk (T) allele acts mainly by impairing glucose-stimulated insulin secretion and blunting the incretin effect, rather than by causing peripheral insulin resistance per se, illustrating that T2D genetic risk is heavily weighted toward beta-cell dysfunction.</p>

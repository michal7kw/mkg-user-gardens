<h1>human_chorionic_gonadotropin_hcg</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Human chorionic gonadotropin (hCG) is a hormone produced by placental trophoblast tissue during pregnancy and by certain tumors. The blood hCG test quantifies hormone levels and is used to confirm and monitor pregnancy, including tracking its progression and detecting complications. Quantitative hCG also serves as a tumor marker for germ-cell tumors and gestational trophoblastic disease.</p>

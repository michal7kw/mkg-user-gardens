<h1>creatine_kinase</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Creatine kinase (CK) is an enzyme found predominantly in skeletal muscle, cardiac muscle, and brain tissue, and it is released into the bloodstream when these tissues are damaged. The serum CK test quantifies total CK activity and is ordered to detect and monitor muscle injury. It is commonly used to evaluate suspected rhabdomyolysis, inflammatory or metabolic myopathies, and muscle-related toxicity from statin medications.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to evaluate and monitor rhabdomyolysis, skeletal and cardiac myopathy, and statin myotoxicity, as well as other causes of muscle breakdown such as trauma, intense exertion, and inflammatory muscle disease.</p>

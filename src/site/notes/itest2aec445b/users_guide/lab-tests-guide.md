<h1>Lab Tests — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>A catalog of lab tests plus tools to track your own results. It has five tabs:<br />
<strong>Catalog</strong>, <strong>Panels</strong>, <strong>My Tests</strong>, <strong>Recommended</strong>, and <strong>Schedule</strong>.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Browse the Catalog</strong> — search by name and filter by category and sample<br />
type. Click a test for detail: reference ranges, clinical significance, and<br />
(for your selected country) availability and how to get it.</li>
<li><strong>Upload a lab report</strong> — import a PDF, CSV, or photo. The parser extracts<br />
results for you to review before they become tracked biomarkers.</li>
<li><strong>Build a panel</strong> under <em>Panels</em> to group tests you order together.</li>
<li><strong>See Recommended</strong> tests suggested from your health goals and out-of-range<br />
markers, and use <em>Schedule</em> to track upcoming tests.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Availability and access pathways change with the country switcher in the top<br />
bar (e.g. NHS vs NFZ vs SSN).</li>
<li>Uploaded results land in a review step first — nothing is tracked until you<br />
confirm it.</li>
</ul>

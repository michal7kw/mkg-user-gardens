<h1>complement_component_c4a</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Complement component C4a is an anaphylatoxin fragment generated when the complement cascade is activated, and it serves as a marker of complement system activation and inflammation. The C4a test measures circulating levels of this fragment in plasma. It is used in the evaluation of complement activation and is among the biomarkers studied in chronic inflammatory response syndrome (CIRS).</p>

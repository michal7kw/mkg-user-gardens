<h1>thyroxine_binding_globulin_tbg</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Thyroxine-binding globulin (TBG) is the principal serum protein responsible for transporting thyroid hormones (T4 and T3) in the bloodstream. Because changes in TBG concentration alter total thyroid hormone levels without affecting the metabolically active free fraction, measuring TBG helps explain discordant results. It is ordered when total T4 or T3 values do not match a patient's clinical thyroid status.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>
<p>This test measures: Thyroxine-binding globulin (TBG).</p>
<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to interpret discordant thyroid function tests, such as when total T4/T3 are abnormal but free hormone levels and clinical status are normal (e.g., congenital TBG excess or deficiency, pregnancy, or estrogen effects).</p>

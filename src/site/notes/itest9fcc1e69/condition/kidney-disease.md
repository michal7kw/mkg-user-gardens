<h1>Kidney Disease</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Kidney disease includes acute kidney injury and chronic kidney disease. <strong>Diabetes and hypertension</strong> account for the majority of cases. Early detection through routine screening allows interventions that slow progression.</p>
<h2>Management</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>ACE inhibitor or ARB</strong> for proteinuria</li>
<li><strong>SGLT2 inhibitor</strong> for diabetic kidney disease</li>
<li><strong>Blood pressure and glycemic control</strong></li>
<li><strong>Renal replacement therapy</strong> for ESRD</li>
</ul>

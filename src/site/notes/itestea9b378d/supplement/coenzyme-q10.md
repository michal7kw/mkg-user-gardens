<h1>Coenzyme Q10</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>A vitamin-like substance found in every cell of the body, essential for mitochondrial ATP production and acting as a powerful lipid-soluble antioxidant.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)</p>
<h2>Mechanism of Action</h2>
<p>Electron carrier in the mitochondrial electron transport chain (Complex I/II to III) facilitating ATP synthesis; membrane antioxidant protecting lipids from peroxidation.</p>
<h2>Dosage</h2>
<p>100-200 mg/day for general health; up to 300-600 mg for heart failure/neuroprotection. Best absorbed with fat (ubiquinol form typically shows superior absorption).</p>
<h2>Biomarker Effects</h2>
<p>Best-evidenced measurable effects (see References — evidence graded on the biomarker edges, not fabricated here):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong><a href="/garden/itestea9b378d/labtest/bnp/" class="internal-link">BNP</a></strong> — reduced in heart failure (Q-SYMBIO RCT; PMID 24444950).</li>
<li>Blood pressure — modest systolic reduction (~3-6 mmHg) in some trials.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/garden/itestea9b378d/labtest/creatine-kinase/" class="internal-link">Creatine Kinase</a></strong> — may attenuate statin-associated elevations and myalgia.</li>
<li><strong><a href="/garden/itestea9b378d/labtest/oxidized-ldl/" class="internal-link">Oxidized LDL</a></strong> — lowered via lipid-phase antioxidant activity.</li>
</ul>

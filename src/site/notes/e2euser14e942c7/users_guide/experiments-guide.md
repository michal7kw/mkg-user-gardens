<h1>Experiments — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>A framework for structured N-of-1 self-experiments — proper single-subject design<br />
to test whether an intervention actually moves your biomarkers.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Create an experiment</strong> and choose the intervention and biomarkers to track.</li>
<li><strong>Move through phases</strong> — Planning → Baseline → Intervention → Washout →<br />
Follow-up → Completed — logging readings as you go.</li>
<li><strong>Read the statistical readout</strong> — once you have enough data, the experiment<br />
computes effect size (Cohen's d) and significance against baseline.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>A baseline phase matters — without it there's nothing to compare the<br />
intervention against.</li>
<li>Keep conditions consistent across phases so the effect you measure is real.</li>
</ul>

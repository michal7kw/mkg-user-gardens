<h1>Dashboard — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Your home base. The dashboard gives you a one-glance view of your health: key<br />
stat cards, a customizable grid of widgets (charts, health score, AI insights,<br />
quick entry), and any alerts that need attention.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Read the stat cards</strong> at the top — tracked biomarkers, active insights,<br />
active recommendations, and health alerts. Click any card to jump to that<br />
section.</li>
<li><strong>Refresh</strong> with the button in the welcome header to re-pull everything from<br />
the server.</li>
<li><strong>Customize widgets</strong> — open <em>Dashboard Preferences</em> to choose which widgets<br />
show, then drag to rearrange and resize them.</li>
<li><strong>Toggle AI recommendations</strong> with the <em>Show AI Recommendations</em> button to<br />
surface a contextual, knowledge-graph-grounded suggestions panel.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Widget layout is saved per user, so it follows you across devices.</li>
<li>Alerts and &quot;active insights&quot; are driven by your biomarker data — the dashboard<br />
is most useful once you've entered or imported some lab results.</li>
</ul>

<h1>syphilis_rpr</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The rapid plasma reagin (RPR) test is a non-treponemal serologic assay that detects antibodies (reagin) produced in response to lipoidal material released by Treponema pallidum-damaged cells. It is widely used for syphilis screening and, because results are reported as a titer, for monitoring response to treatment over time.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used for syphilis screening and for monitoring treatment response; a reactive RPR is typically confirmed with a treponemal-specific test as part of the diagnostic algorithm.</p>

<h1>Systems — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>A way to navigate the knowledge graph through the lens of human biology. Each<br />
biological system gathers the conditions, biomarkers, supplements, genes, and<br />
pathways related to it.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Pick a system</strong> (cardiovascular, nervous, endocrine, …) to see the entities<br />
grouped under it.</li>
<li><strong>Drill into an entity</strong> to open its detail page and relationships.</li>
<li><strong>Cross over to the graph</strong> to see the same entities in the interactive<br />
visualization.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>This is a good starting point when you know the <em>area</em> of interest but not the<br />
specific entity.</li>
</ul>

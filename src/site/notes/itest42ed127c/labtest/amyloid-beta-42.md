<h1>amyloid_beta_42</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test quantifies the 42-amino-acid beta-amyloid peptide (Aβ42). Aβ42 is the most aggregation-prone amyloid-beta species and the primary constituent of cerebral amyloid plaques. Reduced plasma and cerebrospinal fluid levels of Aβ42 reflect sequestration of the peptide into brain plaques, making it a key marker of amyloid pathology in Alzheimer's disease.</p>

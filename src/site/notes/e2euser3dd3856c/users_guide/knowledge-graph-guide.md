<h1>Knowledge Graph — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>An interactive, force-directed view of 1000+ medical entities and their<br />
relationships — supplements, biomarkers, conditions, pathways, and more.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Explore</strong> — drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and click a node to open its detail<br />
panel (description, properties, related entities).</li>
<li><strong>Search</strong> — use the in-page search or the global search bar to centre the<br />
graph on any entity and see its 1-hop relationships.</li>
<li><strong>Filter</strong> — toggle entity types with the colored chips; recolor supplement<br />
nodes by category, target system, mechanism, or evidence level.</li>
<li><strong>Read the About tab</strong> for a reference of entity and relationship types.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Nodes are colored by type (e.g. supplements green, biomarkers blue, conditions<br />
red).</li>
<li>The global search spans the curated graph plus the DrugBank and PrimeKG<br />
reference databases — results are tagged by source.</li>
</ul>

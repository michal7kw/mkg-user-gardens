<h1>Digital Garden — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Tools to publish your curated health knowledge as a public digital garden website<br />
— the same kind of site you're reading now.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Choose what to publish</strong> — select which entities from your knowledge graph go<br />
public, toggling visibility per entity.</li>
<li><strong>Configure mirror settings</strong> to control how your vault content maps to the<br />
published site.</li>
<li><strong>Publish</strong> — selected entities appear on your public garden URL.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Publishing is a paid feature; free-tier users see an upgrade prompt.</li>
<li>Only entities you explicitly mark are published — your private data stays<br />
private by default.</li>
</ul>

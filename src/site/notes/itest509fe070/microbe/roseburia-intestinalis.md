<h1>Roseburia intestinalis</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Major butyrate-producing gut commensal in the Firmicutes phylum (now Bacillota). Typically 1-5% of healthy adult gut microbiota. Consistently depleted in type 2 diabetes, ulcerative colitis, and atherosclerosis. Closely tied to dietary fiber intake — disappears rapidly on low-fiber diets and recovers with fermentable fiber supplementation.</p>

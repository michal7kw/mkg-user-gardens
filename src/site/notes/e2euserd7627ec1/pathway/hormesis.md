<h1>Hormesis</h1>
<h2>Description</h2>
<p>A biological phenomenon where a beneficial effect results from exposure to low doses of an agent that is otherwise toxic or lethal at higher doses. The &quot;what doesn't kill you makes you stronger&quot; mechanism.</p>

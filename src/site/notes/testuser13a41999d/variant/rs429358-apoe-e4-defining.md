<h1>rs429358 (APOE-e4 defining)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Single-nucleotide variant in exon 4 of APOE that converts cysteine to arginine at position 130 (legacy numbering: position 112). The C allele (combined with rs7412 T) defines the APOE-e4 haplotype. Each APOE-e4 allele approximately triples lifetime Alzheimer's disease risk; e4/e4 homozygotes have ~10-15x risk and ~10-year earlier age of onset. Strongest common genetic risk factor for late-onset Alzheimer's disease (Corder et al. 1993).</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)</p>

<h1>lyme_western_blot</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The Lyme Western blot (immunoblot) is the confirmatory second-tier test in the standard two-tier Lyme disease algorithm, performed after a reactive or equivocal first-tier antibody screen. It detects specific IgM and IgG antibodies against multiple Borrelia burgdorferi protein bands, with interpretation based on the number and pattern of reactive bands.</p>

<h3>Conditions</h3>

<h1>rs6025 (Factor V Leiden)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Missense variant in F5 (coagulation factor V) — the classic &quot;Factor V Leiden&quot; mutation, named for the Dutch city where it was discovered (Bertina et al. 1994). Substitutes Arg→Gln at one of the activated protein C (APC) cleavage sites, making activated factor V resistant to APC-mediated degradation. This shifts the coagulation balance toward thrombosis. Heterozygotes have ~5-7x increased venous thromboembolism (VTE) risk; rare homozygotes ~80x. Risk is dramatically amplified by estrogen-containing contraceptives (~35x heterozygote VTE risk on the pill) and pregnancy.</p>
<p><strong>Most common inherited thrombophilia</strong> in people of European ancestry (~3-5% heterozygote frequency); essentially absent in African and East Asian populations.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5) — clinically actionable</p>

<h3>Clinical Actionability</h3>
<ul>

<li><strong>Extended anticoagulation</strong> considered after first unprovoked VTE in homozygotes</li>

<h1>beta_amyloid_42_40_ratio</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test reports the ratio of amyloid-beta 42 to amyloid-beta 40 (Aβ42/Aβ40). Because Aβ42 is preferentially deposited into cerebral amyloid plaques while Aβ40 remains relatively stable, a lower ratio indicates greater plaque burden. Normalizing Aβ42 to Aβ40 controls for individual variation in total amyloid production, making the ratio more reliable than either peptide alone for Alzheimer's disease risk stratification.</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>

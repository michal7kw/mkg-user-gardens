<h1>deamidated_gliadin_igg</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test measures deamidated gliadin peptide IgG (DGP-IgG) antibodies in serum. These antibodies are directed against deamidated forms of gliadin, a component of gluten, and support the diagnosis of celiac disease. DGP-IgG is especially valuable in patients with selective IgA deficiency, in whom standard IgA-based celiac serology is unreliable.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to aid celiac disease diagnosis, particularly in patients with IgA deficiency where IgA-based antibody tests cannot be interpreted reliably.</p>

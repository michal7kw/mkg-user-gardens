<h1>immunoglobulin_g_igg</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test quantifies the total concentration of Immunoglobulin G (IgG) in serum. IgG is the most abundant circulating antibody class and provides the bulk of long-term humoral immunity against pathogens. The test is used to evaluate overall immune competence and to investigate suspected immunodeficiency or chronic infection and inflammation.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>
<p>This test measures: Immunoglobulin G (IgG).</p>
<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used in the workup of immunodeficiency and in the assessment of chronic infection, where altered IgG levels can reflect impaired or sustained immune activation.</p>

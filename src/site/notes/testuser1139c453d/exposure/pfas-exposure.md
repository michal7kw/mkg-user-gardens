<h1>PFAS Exposure</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (&quot;forever chemicals&quot;) are a class of &gt;12,000 synthetic compounds used in non-stick coatings, water-repellent fabrics, food packaging, and firefighting foam since the 1940s. They bioaccumulate in human serum (half-life 2-8 years for major species like PFOA and PFOS) and are detectable in &gt;97% of US blood samples. Strongly linked to increased cholesterol, kidney/testicular cancer, thyroid dysfunction, immune suppression, and reduced vaccine response. EPA finalized enforceable drinking-water MCLs for 6 PFAS in April 2024 — utilities must comply by 2029.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) — strong epidemiological signal across multiple cohorts; mechanism still being characterized</p>

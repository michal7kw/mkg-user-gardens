<h1>Walnuts</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Tree nut from Juglans regia. Distinctive among nuts for high alpha-linolenic acid (ALA, the plant-source omega-3) — about 9 g per 100g, the highest of any common food. Also rich in manganese, copper, biotin, magnesium, and polyphenols including ellagitannins (precursors to urolithins, gut-microbiome-derived metabolites associated with mitochondrial benefits via mitophagy induction).</p>
<p>PREDIMED-Nuts arm (the trial in <a href="/garden/itestd8a1872d/research/predimed-2018-mediterranean-diet-for-primary-cv-prevention/" class="internal-link">PREDIMED 2018 Mediterranean Diet</a>) used 30g of mixed nuts per day (15g walnuts + 7.5g almonds + 7.5g hazelnuts) and showed ~28% relative reduction in major CV events vs the reduced-fat control.</p>
<p>ALA → EPA conversion in humans is inefficient (~5-10%); walnuts complement but do not substitute for marine omega-3 sources.</p>

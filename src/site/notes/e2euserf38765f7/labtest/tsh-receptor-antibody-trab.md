<h1>tsh_receptor_antibody_trab</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>TSH receptor antibodies (TRAb) are autoantibodies directed against the thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor and are the hallmark of autoimmune thyroid disease. The assay detects antibodies that may stimulate, block, or bind the receptor, making it a sensitive and specific diagnostic marker. It is also used during pregnancy to assess the risk of fetal and neonatal thyroid dysfunction.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to diagnose Graves' disease and other autoimmune thyroid disease, and to assess pregnancy-related risk of neonatal thyroid dysfunction.</p>

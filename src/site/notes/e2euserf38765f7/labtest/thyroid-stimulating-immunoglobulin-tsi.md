<h1>thyroid_stimulating_immunoglobulin_tsi</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Thyroid-stimulating immunoglobulin (TSI) is an autoantibody that binds to and activates the TSH receptor on thyroid cells, driving excess thyroid hormone production. The test specifically detects the stimulating subset of TSH receptor antibodies and is highly specific for autoimmune hyperthyroidism. It is ordered to confirm the underlying cause of hyperthyroidism.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to confirm Graves' disease as the cause of hyperthyroidism, particularly when clinical or imaging findings are ambiguous.</p>

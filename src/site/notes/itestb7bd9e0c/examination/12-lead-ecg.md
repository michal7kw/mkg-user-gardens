<h1>12-Lead ECG</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Twelve-lead electrocardiogram recording the heart's electrical activity from 12 vectorial perspectives (6 limb leads + 6 precordial leads). First-line test for chest pain, palpitations, syncope, and suspected cardiac ischemia, arrhythmia, or conduction disease. Critical decision tool in the ED — STEMI must be identified within 10 minutes of arrival per AHA guidelines.</p>
<h2>Procedure Details</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Modality:</strong> Electrophysiology</li>
<li><strong>Invasiveness:</strong> Non-invasive</li>
<li><strong>Typical Duration:</strong> 5 minutes</li>
<li><strong>Body Sites:</strong> heart</li>
</ul>
<h2>Preparation</h2>
<ul>
<li>No special preparation required</li>
<li>Avoid lotions on chest skin (interferes with electrode adhesion)</li>
</ul>
<h2>Clinical Use</h2>
<h3>Typical Indications</h3>
<ul>
<li>Chest pain (rule out acute coronary syndrome)</li>
<li>Palpitations</li>
<li>Syncope or pre-syncope</li>
<li>Suspected arrhythmia</li>
<li>Pre-operative cardiac assessment</li>
</ul>

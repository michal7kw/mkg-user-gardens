<h1>Colonoscopy</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Endoscopic visualization of the entire colon and terminal ileum using a flexible fiber-optic instrument. Gold-standard screening test for colorectal cancer; allows simultaneous polyp removal (polypectomy) which is both diagnostic (histology) and therapeutic (cancer prevention). USPSTF (2021) recommends screening every 10 years from age 45 for average-risk individuals; earlier and more frequent for those with family history, inflammatory bowel disease, or Lynch syndrome.</p>

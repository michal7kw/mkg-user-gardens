<h1>Cognitive Flexibility</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Cognitive flexibility is the capacity to switch perspectives, update rules, and adapt behavior when circumstances change — to &quot;think outside the box&quot; and shift gears. In Adele Diamond's influential framework it is one of the three core executive functions (alongside working memory and inhibitory control) from which higher-order abilities such as reasoning and problem-solving are built (see <a href="/garden/itest40300619/cognitivefunction/executive-function/" class="internal-link">Executive Function</a>). It encompasses both task-switching (set-shifting) and the ability to consider multiple representations of the same situation.</p>
<p>Cognitive flexibility relies on the dynamic interplay of prefrontal cortex and basal ganglia, tuned by <a href="/garden/itest40300619/neurotransmitter/dopamine/" class="internal-link">Dopamine</a> — the same inverted-U relationship that governs other executive functions, where both too little and too much dopaminergic tone impair flexible switching.</p>
<h2>Neural Basis</h2>
<p>Set-shifting engages the ventrolateral and dorsolateral <a class="internal-link is-unresolved" href="/404">Prefrontal Cortex</a>, anterior cingulate cortex (conflict monitoring), and frontostriatal loops through the basal ganglia. <a href="/garden/itest40300619/neurotransmitter/dopamine/" class="internal-link">Dopamine</a> and <a href="/garden/itest40300619/neurotransmitter/serotonin/" class="internal-link">Serotonin</a> both modulate flexibility; striatal dopamine supports the gating of rule updates.</p>
<h2>Clinical Relevance</h2>
<p>Reduced cognitive flexibility (perseveration, rigidity) is characteristic of <a href="/garden/itest40300619/condition/attention-deficit-hyperactivity-disorder/" class="internal-link">Attention Deficit Hyperactivity Disorder</a>, obsessive-compulsive disorder, autism, <a href="/garden/itest40300619/condition/depression/" class="internal-link">Depression</a>, and frontostriatal neurodegeneration (e.g., Parkinson's). It is assessed by perseverative errors on the Wisconsin Card Sorting Test.</p>

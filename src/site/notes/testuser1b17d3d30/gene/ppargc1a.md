<h1>PPARGC1A</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>PPARGC1A encodes <strong>PGC-1alpha</strong> (peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor gamma coactivator 1-alpha), the master regulator of <a href="/garden/testuser1b17d3d30/pathway/mitochondrial-biogenesis/" class="internal-link">Mitochondrial Biogenesis</a> and oxidative metabolism. PGC-1alpha is not a transcription factor itself but a coactivator that docks onto nuclear receptors and factors (PPARs, NRF1/2, ERRalpha) to switch on the broad program of mitochondrial and oxidative genes. Its activity is induced by the cell's energy sensors — AMPK (phosphorylation) and SIRT1 (deacetylation) — making PPARGC1A the genetic hub through which <a href="/garden/testuser1b17d3d30/intervention/exercise/" class="internal-link">Exercise</a>, cold, and fasting remodel metabolism.</p>
<p>The common Gly482Ser (rs8192678) variant has been studied for associations with type 2 diabetes susceptibility and inter-individual differences in trainability and metabolic response.</p>

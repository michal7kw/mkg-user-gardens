<h1>vitamin_a_retinol</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Measures serum retinol, the circulating form of Vitamin A, a fat-soluble vitamin essential for vision, immune function, and epithelial integrity. The test evaluates vitamin A deficiency, which can cause night blindness and impaired immunity, as well as toxicity from excessive intake.</p>

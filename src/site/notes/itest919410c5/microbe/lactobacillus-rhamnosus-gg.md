<h1>Lactobacillus rhamnosus GG</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The most extensively studied probiotic strain (LGG, isolated by Gorbach and Goldin in 1985, hence &quot;GG&quot;). Strong evidence for prevention of antibiotic-associated diarrhea, traveler's diarrhea, atopic dermatitis in children, and reduction of duration of acute pediatric gastroenteritis. The strain is now formally classified under the genus <em>Lacticaseibacillus</em> (renamed from <em>Lactobacillus</em> in 2020).</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)</p>

<li><strong>NCBI Taxonomy ID:</strong> <a href="https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/Taxonomy/Browser/wwwtax.cgi?id=568703">568703</a></li>

<h2>Habitat</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Body Sites:</strong> gut, oral, vaginal (transient colonizer)</li>
<li><strong>Oxygen Requirement:</strong> Facultative anaerobe (aerotolerant)</li>
</ul>
<h2>Clinical Role</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pathogenicity:</strong> Beneficial commensal (rare bloodstream infections in severely immunocompromised patients)</li>
<li><strong>Key Metabolites Produced:</strong> lactate, hydrogen peroxide, bacteriocins</li>
</ul>
<p>Adheres tightly to intestinal epithelium via SpaCBA pili; inhibits enteric pathogen adhesion; modulates TLR signaling; reinforces tight junctions.</p>

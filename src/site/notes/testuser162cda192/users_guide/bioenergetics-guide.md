<h1>Bioenergetics — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>A view into how your body produces cellular energy — from glucose breakdown to<br />
ATP synthesis. It has four tabs: <strong>Pathway Explorer</strong>, <strong>Metabolic Health</strong>,<br />
<strong>Energy Balance</strong>, and <strong>Interventions</strong>.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Explore pathways</strong> — the interactive diagram shows metabolites, enzymes, and<br />
complexes (glycolysis, TCA cycle, electron transport chain, beta-oxidation).<br />
Click a node for its knowledge-graph context.</li>
<li><strong>Check your metabolic score</strong> (0–100) under Metabolic Health.</li>
<li><strong>Overlay your data</strong> — use <em>Show My Data</em> to color pathway nodes by your<br />
biomarker values (normal / suboptimal / dysfunctional).</li>
<li><strong>See interventions</strong> — the Interventions tab detects dysfunction patterns and<br />
recommends targeted supplements with evidence levels and dosages.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Overlays need your biomarker data — enter readings first for the personalised<br />
view.</li>
<li>For exercise-specific energy metabolism, see the Energy Systems page.</li>
</ul>

<h1>phosphorus</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The serum phosphorus test measures the concentration of inorganic phosphate in the blood, a mineral essential for bone mineralization, energy metabolism, and cell-membrane structure. Phosphate levels are tightly regulated by the kidneys, parathyroid hormone, and vitamin D. The test is ordered as part of bone and mineral metabolism evaluation and in the assessment of renal function and related disorders.</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to evaluate bone-mineral disorders, chronic kidney disease (CKD), and parathyroid disorders, as well as to investigate abnormal calcium levels and disturbances of phosphate balance.</p>

<h1>herpes_simplex_virus_igg</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This serologic test detects type-specific IgG antibodies against herpes simplex virus type 1 (HSV-1) and type 2 (HSV-2), using glycoprotein G-based assays to distinguish the two types. A positive result indicates prior exposure and established infection rather than the timing of acquisition.</p>

<h2>Biomarkers Measured</h2>
<p>This test measures: HSV-1/HSV-2 IgG.</p>
<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used for herpes exposure assessment, including evaluation of individuals with a history suggestive of genital or orolabial herpes and partners of infected individuals.</p>

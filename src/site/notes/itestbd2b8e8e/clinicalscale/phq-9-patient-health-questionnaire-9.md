<h1>PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Nine-item self-report depression screening instrument scored 0-27, validated against DSM-IV major depressive disorder criteria (Kroenke et al. 2001). Each item maps directly to one of the nine DSM criteria for MDD. Standard cutoff of ≥10 has sensitivity 88% and specificity 88% for major depression. Item 9 (&quot;thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself in some way&quot;) provides automatic suicidality screening — any non-zero response on item 9 mandates clinical follow-up regardless of total score.</p>
<p>The PHQ-9 is in the public domain (released by Pfizer) and is used in primary care, mental health, and research worldwide. It is suitable for both initial screening and longitudinal treatment monitoring.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)</p>

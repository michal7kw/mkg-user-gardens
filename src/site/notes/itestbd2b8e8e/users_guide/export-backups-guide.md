<h1>Export &amp; Backups — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>Tools to move your health data in and out of MKG and to keep backups. It has<br />
tabs for <strong>Import</strong>, <strong>Export</strong>, <strong>Backups</strong>, and <strong>Batch Jobs</strong>.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Import</strong> lab reports (PDF / CSV) to bring results into the platform.</li>
<li><strong>Export</strong> your data as JSON, CSV, or PDF for portability or sharing.</li>
<li><strong>Back up and restore</strong> your full data set under Backups.</li>
<li><strong>Monitor Batch Jobs</strong> to track long-running import/processing tasks.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Exports are a quick way to take your data to another tool or a clinician.</li>
<li>Keep a periodic backup, especially before large imports or bulk edits.</li>
</ul>

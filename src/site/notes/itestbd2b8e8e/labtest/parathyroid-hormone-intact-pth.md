<h1>parathyroid_hormone_intact_pth</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Intact parathyroid hormone (PTH) is the full-length, biologically active hormone secreted by the parathyroid glands to regulate calcium and phosphate balance. The intact PTH assay measures this active form in blood and is central to interpreting abnormal calcium levels. It is ordered to distinguish causes of hypercalcemia and hypocalcemia, to diagnose hyper- and hypoparathyroidism, and to manage chronic kidney disease-mineral and bone disorder (CKD-MBD).</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used to evaluate hyperparathyroidism and hypoparathyroidism, abnormal calcium levels, and chronic kidney disease-mineral and bone disorder (CKD-MBD), typically interpreted alongside calcium and vitamin D results.</p>

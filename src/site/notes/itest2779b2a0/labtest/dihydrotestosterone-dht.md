<h1>dihydrotestosterone_dht</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Dihydrotestosterone (DHT) is the most potent endogenous androgen, formed from testosterone by the enzyme 5α-reductase in peripheral tissues such as the skin, hair follicles, and prostate. Measuring serum DHT helps assess androgen activity at the tissue level and the function of the 5α-reductase pathway. It is ordered when conditions of androgen excess or deficiency in DHT conversion are suspected.</p>

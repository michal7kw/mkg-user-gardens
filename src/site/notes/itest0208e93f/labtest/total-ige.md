<h1>total_ige</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test measures the total concentration of immunoglobulin E (IgE) in serum. IgE is the antibody class central to type I hypersensitivity reactions, and elevated total levels are associated with atopy, allergic diseases such as asthma and allergic rhinitis, and parasitic infections.</p>

<h1>rs762551 (CYP1A2 *1F)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>rs762551 is a regulatory single-nucleotide polymorphism in the first intron of the <a href="/garden/e2euser31452fcf/protein/cyp1a2/" class="internal-link">CYP1A2</a> gene, whose enzyme metabolizes roughly 95% of ingested caffeine. The A allele (the *1F haplotype) confers higher enzyme inducibility and faster caffeine clearance: AA homozygotes are &quot;fast metabolizers,&quot; whereas carriers of the C allele clear caffeine more slowly. This single variant is the basis of much consumer &quot;caffeine genetics,&quot; though the real-world effect sizes are moderate and context-dependent.</p>

</ul>
<h2>Clinical Significance</h2>
<p>Genotype modifies the relationship between coffee intake and cardiovascular risk: in slow metabolizers (C carriers), higher coffee consumption has been associated with increased risk of hypertension and myocardial infarction, whereas fast metabolizers appear protected or unaffected. Genotype also influences the ergogenic response to caffeine in sport.</p>

<h1>LDL Medium</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>LDL particles come in different sizes—small, medium, and large. Medium particles tend to stay in circulation longer than large ones, which may increase the chance of cholesterol building up in artery walls and raising the risk of atherosclerosis and cardiovascular disease.</p>

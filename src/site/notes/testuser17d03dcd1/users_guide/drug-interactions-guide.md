<h1>Drug Interactions — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>A safety-critical checker for drug–drug and drug–supplement interactions, backed<br />
by the DrugBank database.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Review the auto-loaded check</strong> of your active medications.</li>
<li><strong>Add drugs manually</strong> — search by name and select 2–10 to check together.</li>
<li><strong>Run the check</strong> and read results, color-coded by severity: red<br />
(major / contraindicated), orange (moderate), yellow (minor).</li>
<li><strong>Click a drug name</strong> to open its detail page (pharmacology, targets, enzymes,<br />
categories).</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Always check before combining medications or adding a new supplement to your<br />
stack.</li>
<li>This is a decision-support tool, not a substitute for your pharmacist or<br />
doctor.</li>
</ul>

<h1>arsenic</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Measures arsenic exposure, with urine being the preferred specimen for detecting recent exposure. The test helps assess both acute and chronic toxicity arising from environmental or occupational sources such as contaminated water, certain foods, and industrial processes.</p>

<h2>Recommended For</h2>
<h3>Conditions</h3>
<p>Used for evaluating toxic metal exposure and in environmental toxicology investigations of suspected acute or chronic arsenic contamination.</p>

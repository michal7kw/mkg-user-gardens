<h1>Supplement Map — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>An interactive anatomy explorer showing how supplements affect your body, organ<br />
by organ.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Explore the body</strong> — organs are highlighted by your supplement stack. Click<br />
an organ to see which supplements affect it.</li>
<li><strong>Use the pills</strong> — green pills are supplements, gold pills are biomarkers.<br />
Click a pill to expand it; click <em>Details</em> for the full modal (mechanism,<br />
dosage, molecular structure, interactions).</li>
<li><strong>Filter</strong> by body system (cardiovascular, nervous, digestive, …), toggle<br />
biomarker pills, and adjust pill density.</li>
<li><strong>Search</strong> for a specific supplement to focus the map.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Hover a pill to see connection lines to the organs it affects.</li>
<li>The map reflects your stack — add supplements under <em>My Stack</em> to populate it.</li>
</ul>

<h1>lyme_antibody_screen</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The Lyme antibody screen is a first-tier enzyme immunoassay (ELISA/EIA) that detects antibodies to Borrelia burgdorferi, the bacterium that causes Lyme disease. It serves as the initial step in the standard two-tier testing algorithm; reactive or equivocal results are confirmed by a second-tier test.</p>

<h3>Conditions</h3>

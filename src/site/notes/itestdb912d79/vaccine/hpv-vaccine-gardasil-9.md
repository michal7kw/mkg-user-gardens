<h1>HPV Vaccine (Gardasil 9)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>9-valent recombinant HPV virus-like particle vaccine covering HPV types 6, 11, 16, 18, 31, 33, 45, 52, 58. Together these cover ~90% of cervical cancers and ~80% of HPV-associated anal, oropharyngeal, penile, vulvar, and vaginal cancers. Lei et al. (NEJM 2020) using Swedish national registry data showed &gt;88% reduction in cervical cancer incidence among women vaccinated before age 17.</p>
<p>ACIP recommendation: routine 2-dose schedule at age 11-12 (catch-up through 26; shared decision-making 27-45). Two doses if started before age 15; three doses otherwise. Both sexes.</p>

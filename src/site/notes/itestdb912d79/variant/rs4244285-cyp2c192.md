<h1>rs4244285 (CYP2C19*2)</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Loss-of-function splice-site variant defining the CYP2C19*2 allele — the most common reduced-function CYP2C19 variant globally. The A allele creates an aberrant splice site that produces a truncated, non-functional enzyme. Carriers are intermediate metabolizers (*1/*2) or poor metabolizers (*2/*2) of clopidogrel, omeprazole, escitalopram, voriconazole, and other CYP2C19 substrates. FDA issued a boxed warning for clopidogrel in poor metabolizers post-PCI (2010) — these patients have reduced platelet inhibition and higher rates of stent thrombosis on standard-dose clopidogrel.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5) — PharmGKB Level 1A for multiple drugs</p>

<h3>Clinical Actionability</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>CPIC guidelines</strong> exist for clopidogrel, voriconazole, SSRIs, and PPIs</li>
<li><strong>Alternative for clopidogrel poor metabolizers post-PCI:</strong> prasugrel or ticagrelor</li>
<li><strong>SSRI poor metabolizers:</strong> start at 50% of usual dose</li>
</ul>

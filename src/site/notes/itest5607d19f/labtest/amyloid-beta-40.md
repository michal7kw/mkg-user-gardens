<h1>amyloid_beta_40</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>This test quantifies the 40-amino-acid beta-amyloid peptide (Aβ40) in plasma. Aβ40 is the most abundant amyloid-beta species and is measured alongside Aβ42 to derive the Aβ42/40 ratio, which improves the accuracy of Alzheimer's disease risk assessment by normalizing for individual differences in total amyloid production. It is typically ordered as part of a cognitive decline workup.</p>

<h3>Conditions</h3>

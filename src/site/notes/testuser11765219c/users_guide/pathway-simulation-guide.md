<h1>Pathway Simulation — Guide</h1>
<h2>What this page is</h2>
<p>An experimental tool that runs ODE-based kinetic simulations of metabolic<br />
pathways, so you can test how a supplement changes metabolite concentrations over<br />
time.</p>
<h2>Key actions</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Select a pathway</strong> (e.g. Creatine, Dopamine, Glutathione) in the left panel.</li>
<li><strong>Add interventions</strong> — supplements with doses — and click <em>Run Simulation</em>.</li>
<li><strong>Read the results</strong> across five tabs: Time Series, Comparison, Pathway<br />
Network, Dose-Response, and Sensitivity.</li>
<li><strong>Compare scenarios</strong> side by side to test multiple supplement strategies.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Tips &amp; gotchas</h2>
<ul>
<li>Simulations are models, not predictions of your individual response — treat<br />
them as hypotheses to explore.</li>
<li>Adjust time span and initial conditions in advanced options for finer control.</li>
</ul>

<h1>DEXA Scan</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>Dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry. Gold-standard test for bone mineral density (osteoporosis screening at hip and lumbar spine) and increasingly used for whole-body composition (lean mass, fat mass, visceral adipose tissue). Reports T-scores (compared to young-adult reference) and Z-scores (age-matched). T-score ≤ -2.5 defines osteoporosis; -1.0 to -2.5 defines osteopenia (WHO criteria). FRAX 10-year fracture risk calculator integrates BMD with clinical factors.</p>
<p>Whole-body DEXA for body composition is gaining popularity in longevity and sports medicine — direct measurement of fat-free mass, regional fat distribution, and visceral adipose tissue, with much higher precision than bioelectrical impedance.</p>

<h1>Serum PFAS Panel</h1>
<h2>Overview</h2>
<p>The Serum PFAS Panel quantifies the blood concentrations of multiple per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) — a large class of synthetic &quot;forever chemicals&quot; that bioaccumulate in human serum with half-lives of several years. Because PFAS persist in the body and integrate exposure over long periods, serum measurement is the standard approach to <strong>human biomonitoring</strong> of cumulative PFAS body burden, rather than estimating intake at a single point in time.</p>
<p>The panel is typically analyzed by liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry (<strong>LC-MS/MS</strong>), which provides sensitive, compound-specific quantification (in ng/mL) of individual species. Commonly reported analytes include perfluorooctanoic acid (<strong>PFOA</strong>), perfluorooctanesulfonic acid (<strong>PFOS</strong>), perfluorohexanesulfonic acid (<strong>PFHxS</strong>), and perfluorononanoic acid (<strong>PFNA</strong>), often summed into a total PFAS burden.</p>
<p><strong>Evidence Level:</strong> ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) — well-validated analytical methodology; clinical interpretation guidance (e.g., NASEM 2022) is evolving.</p>

<h2>Fasting Requirements</h2>
<p>No fasting is required. PFAS reflect cumulative body burden, so the result is not meaningfully affected by recent food or drink.</p>
<h2>Preparation Instructions</h2>
<ol>
<li>No fasting required</li>
<li>Provide occupational and environmental exposure history (e.g., contaminated drinking water, firefighting foam)</li>
<li>Standard venipuncture</li>
</ol>
<h2>Clinical Interpretation</h2>
<p>Per NASEM (2022) guidance, summed serum PFAS concentrations are interpreted in tiers (e.g., &lt; 2 ng/mL low concern; 2-20 ng/mL potential for adverse effects warranting clinical follow-up; &gt; 20 ng/mL increased risk warranting additional screening). Elevated levels may prompt screening for dyslipidemia, thyroid dysfunction, and other associated outcomes, and counseling on exposure reduction.</p>
